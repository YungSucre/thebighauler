Choosing the right business fuel card can save you thousands per year, but the wrong one can cost you in fees and missed discounts. This guide compares the top fuel cards for truckers in 2026, focusing on real costs, benefits, and who each card suits best. We'll break down fees, discounts, and features so you can make an informed decision this week.

## How Fuel Cards Work

Fuel cards are specialized payment cards that let you pay for diesel at truck stops and fuel networks. They offer two main benefits: cost control and discounts. Most cards provide a per-gallon discount off the cash price, and some offer discounts on repairs, lodging, or scales. You get a monthly statement that separates fuel purchases from other expenses, which simplifies bookkeeping and IFTA reporting.

## Top Fuel Card Options in 2026

Here are the most popular business fuel cards for owner-operators and small fleets. Fees and discounts vary by region and volume, so always confirm current terms.

### 1. Comdata

Comdata is one of the largest fuel card networks, accepted at over 8,000 locations including TA, Petro, and Love's. It offers a per-gallon discount that ranges from $0.05 to $0.30 depending on volume and location. There is no monthly fee for the basic plan, but you may pay a transaction fee of $0.15 to $0.25 per purchase. Comdata provides robust reporting, fuel tax details, and a mobile app. It's best for fleets that want wide acceptance and detailed data.

### 2. EFS (WEX)

EFS, now part of WEX, is another major player with a network of over 7,000 locations. Discounts range from $0.03 to $0.20 per gallon. The card has no monthly fee, but transaction fees apply at $0.10 to $0.20. EFS offers strong integration with accounting software and excellent customer support. It's a solid choice for small fleets that need reliable reporting.

### 3. Fleet One (WEX)

Fleet One, also under WEX, is designed for small fleets and owner-operators. It offers discounts of $0.03 to $0.15 per gallon at over 7,000 locations. There is no monthly fee, but transaction fees are $0.10 to $0.15. Fleet One provides a simple mobile app and free fuel locator. It's a good entry-level option.

### 4. T-Chek (FleetCor)

T-Chek is popular among owner-operators, especially those hauling freight for brokers. It offers discounts of $0.05 to $0.25 per gallon at over 6,000 locations. There is a monthly fee of $5 to $10, but transaction fees are lower at $0.05 to $0.10. T-Chek also provides factoring services, which can be handy if you use freight factoring. It's best for drivers who want integrated factoring.

### 5. Fuelman (FleetCor)

Fuelman is another FleetCor brand with a network of over 5,000 locations. Discounts range from $0.04 to $0.20 per gallon. Monthly fees are $5 to $15, and transaction fees are $0.10 to $0.20. Fuelman offers a fuel finder app and customizable spending controls. It's good for fleets that need strict driver controls.

### 6. WEX Fleet Card

WEX's own fleet card is accepted at over 95% of fuel stations in the U.S., including major truck stops. Discounts vary widely, from $0.02 to $0.15 per gallon. There is no monthly fee, but transaction fees are $0.10 to $0.20. The main advantage is the huge acceptance network, which is great for drivers who travel off the beaten path.

## How to Choose the Right Card

Follow these steps to pick the best card for your operation:

1. **Estimate your monthly fuel spend.** Calculate how many gallons you buy per month. Multiply by your average fuel price to get your total spend.
2. **Compare discounts and fees.** For each card, estimate your monthly savings: (gallons × discount) minus fees. For example, if you buy 2,000 gallons and get a $0.10 discount, that's $200 saved. If the card has a $10 monthly fee and $0.15 transaction fee on 20 transactions ($3), your net savings is $187.
3. **Check acceptance at your usual stops.** If you fuel at a specific chain, make sure the card is accepted there. Use the card's locator tool.
4. **Review reporting features.** If you need IFTA reports, look for cards that provide fuel tax details automatically.
5. **Consider extra perks.** Some cards offer discounts on repairs, lodging, or scales. Factor those in if you use them.

## Real-World Cost Example

Let's say you're an owner-operator buying 1,500 gallons per month at $3.50/gallon. You're comparing Comdata and T-Chek.

- Comdata: discount $0.15/gal, no monthly fee, $0.20 transaction fee on 15 transactions ($3). Savings: 1,500 × $0.15 = $225, minus $3 = $222.
- T-Chek: discount $0.10/gal, $10 monthly fee, $0.10 transaction fee on 15 transactions ($1.50). Savings: 1,500 × $0.10 = $150, minus $11.50 = $138.50.

In this case, Comdata saves you more. But if you use factoring, T-Chek might be worth it.

## FAQ

### Do fuel cards affect my credit score?

Most fuel cards are not credit cards; they are prepaid or charge cards. Applying may involve a soft credit check, but it won't hurt your score. However, some cards offer credit terms, which could affect it.

### Can I use a fuel card for other expenses?

Yes, many cards allow purchases like repairs, lodging, and scales. But those may not earn fuel discounts, and some have separate fee structures.

### Are fuel cards worth it for owner-operators?

Yes, if you buy enough fuel. Even a $0.05 discount on 1,000 gallons saves $50 per month, which covers most fees. Just compare net savings.

### How do I get a fuel card?

Apply online through the card provider's website. You'll need your business information, driver's license, and possibly your CDL. Approval usually takes a few days.

## The Bottom Line

No single fuel card is best for everyone. Your choice depends on your fuel volume, preferred stops, and need for extra services like factoring. Start by calculating your monthly fuel spend and comparing net savings. Test a card for a few months, then adjust. The right card can save you $100 to $300 per month, which adds up to $1,200 to $3,600 a year. That's real money for your bottom line.
