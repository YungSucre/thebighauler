## What the Vermont CDL Written Test Covers

The Vermont DMV CDL written test is based on the Commercial Driver License Manual. The general knowledge test includes 50 questions, and you need at least 40 correct answers (80%) to pass. Endorsement tests vary: for example, the Hazmat test has 30 questions with a passing score of 24 (80%).

Key areas include: safe driving practices, vehicle inspection, cargo handling, air brakes, and road rules. Vermont also requires a specific number of hours of behind-the-wheel training before you can take the skills test, but the written test is the first hurdle.

## How to Use Practice Tests Effectively

To get the most out of your practice tests, follow these steps:

1. **Start with the official Vermont DMV test** to gauge your baseline. Take it once without studying to see where you stand.
2. **Use a comprehensive paid test** (like Driving-Tests.org or CDL Practice Test 2026) for daily practice. Aim for at least 30 minutes a day.
3. **Focus on your weak areas.** If you consistently miss air brake questions, review that chapter in the manual and retake the test.
4. **Take timed tests** to simulate real exam conditions. The actual test is timed, so practice pacing.
5. **Review explanations** for every question, even the ones you got right. This reinforces the rules.

## What to Expect on Test Day

On the day of your written test, bring your permit, proof of identity, and payment for the fee (around $30 for the written test, but check current fees). You'll take the test on a computer at a DMV office. You can take the general knowledge test and endorsements in one session, but you must pass general knowledge first. If you fail, you can retake it after 24 hours, but you'll pay the fee again.

## FAQ

### 1. Are free CDL practice tests enough to pass?

Free tests can help you get familiar with the format, but they often lack the depth and variety of paid tests. For the best chance, combine free tests with a paid option that offers detailed explanations and all endorsements.

### 2. How many questions are on the Vermont CDL written test?

The general knowledge test has 50 questions, and you need 40 correct to pass. Endorsement tests have fewer questions, like 20 for air brakes, but the passing score is still 80%.

### 3. Can I take the CDL written test online in Vermont?

No, Vermont requires you to take the written test in person at a DMV office. You cannot take it online, but you can use online practice tests to prepare.

### 4. How much does the CDL written test cost in Vermont?

The written test fee is approximately $30, but it may vary. Check the Vermont DMV website for the most current fee schedule.
