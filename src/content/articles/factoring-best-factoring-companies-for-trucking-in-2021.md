Factoring your freight invoices is a quick way to get cash for fuel, repairs, and payroll without waiting 30 to 60 days for customers to pay. In 2021, the best factoring companies for trucking offer low rates, fast funding, and no hidden fees. This guide breaks down the top options and what to look for so you can make a smart choice.

## What to Look for in a Factoring Company

Before comparing companies, know the basics. Factoring companies buy your invoices at a discount, usually 1% to 5% of the invoice value. You get an advance, typically 80% to 95%, within 24 hours. The factor collects from your customer, then pays you the remaining balance minus the fee.

Key factors to compare:

- **Advance rate**: How much you get upfront. Higher is better, but watch the fee.
- **Discount rate**: The percentage charged per invoice. Ranges from 0.5% to 5% depending on volume and creditworthiness.
- **Contract terms**: Some factors require a minimum volume or a long-term contract. Others are month-to-month.
- **Additional fees**: Look for application fees, monthly minimums, or termination fees. Avoid companies with hidden costs.
- **Customer support**: You want a factor that handles collections professionally, since they deal with your customers.
- **Integration**: Does it work with your dispatch or accounting software? Some factors offer fuel cards or load boards as perks.

## How to Choose the Right Factor for You

1. **Assess your needs**: How much cash do you need weekly? How many invoices do you factor per month? This will help you compare rates and minimums.
2. **Check the fee structure**: Ask for a sample quote. Calculate the effective cost for your average invoice amount and frequency.
3. **Read the contract**: Look for hidden fees like termination fees or monthly minimums. Some factors charge a fee if you don't factor a certain volume.
4. **Test customer service**: Call the company and see how they treat you. You want a factor that will handle your customers professionally.
5. **Consider integration**: If you use a TMS or accounting software, check if the factor integrates. This saves time.

## FAQ

### What is the average factoring rate for trucking?

Rates typically range from 1% to 5% per month, depending on invoice volume, customer credit, and contract terms. Larger fleets with high volume can negotiate lower rates.

### How fast can I get funding?

Most top factors offer same-day funding if you submit invoices before a cutoff time, often 12:00 PM or 2:00 PM ET. Some can fund within hours.

### Are there any hidden fees?

Some factors charge application fees, monthly minimums, or termination fees. Always read the contract and ask for a full fee schedule before signing.

### Can I factor invoices from multiple customers?

Yes, you can factor invoices from any customer, as long as they are creditworthy. Factors will run credit checks on your customers and may decline if the customer has poor credit.
