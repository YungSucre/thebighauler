If you're in Multan during Ramadan and looking for a hearty iftar buffet, you have plenty of options. This guide covers the best buffets in the city, with realistic price ranges and what to expect at each spot. Whether you're a local or just passing through, these picks offer good value and authentic flavors.

## 1. Pearl Continental Hotel Multan

PC Multan is a top choice for a premium iftar buffet. They offer a lavish spread with both local and continental dishes. Expect live cooking stations, a variety of dates, fresh juices, and traditional Pakistani items like pakoras, samosas, and chana chaat. The ambiance is upscale, perfect for families or business gatherings.

- **Price range:** PKR 2,500 to PKR 3,500 per person (2026)
- **Location:** LMQ Road, Multan
- **Highlights:** Extensive dessert section, live BBQ, and a dedicated kids' corner.
- **Reservation:** Recommended, especially on weekends. Call ahead to confirm iftar timing.

## 2. Ramada by Wyndham Multan

Ramada offers a well-balanced iftar buffet that combines traditional Pakistani cuisine with Middle Eastern and continental options. Their spread includes lentil soup, fruit chaat, dahi baray, and a variety of grilled meats. The service is attentive, and the setting is comfortable.

- **Price range:** PKR 2,000 to PKR 2,800 per person
- **Location:** Abdali Road, Multan
- **Highlights:** Freshly prepared jalebis, a good selection of mocktails, and a quiet dining area.
- **Tip:** Check their Ramadan specials; they often include free iftar for kids under a certain age.

## 3. The Nishat Hotel Multan

A more budget-friendly option without compromising on quality, The Nishat Hotel offers a solid iftar buffet with a focus on local flavors. You'll find classic items like haleem, nihari, and a variety of fried snacks. The dessert counter includes traditional sweets like gulab jamun and kheer.

- **Price range:** PKR 1,500 to PKR 2,200 per person
- **Location:** Abdali Road, near DHA
- **Highlights:** Value for money, generous portions, and a family-friendly atmosphere.
- **Note:** They have a separate family section, which is a plus for privacy.

## 4. Bundu Khan Multan

If you prefer a more casual setting, Bundu Khan is a popular restaurant chain that offers an iftar buffet during Ramadan. It's known for its BBQ and traditional Pakistani dishes. The buffet is smaller than hotel spreads but still satisfying.

- **Price range:** PKR 1,200 to PKR 1,800 per person
- **Location:** Multiple branches in Multan (e.g., on Vihari Road)
- **Highlights:** Excellent BBQ items, including seekh kebabs and chicken tikka.
- **Crowd:** Can get busy, so arrive early to avoid long waits.

## 5. Al-Madina Restaurant

For a truly local experience, Al-Madina Restaurant offers a no-frills iftar buffet that's popular among residents. It's not fancy, but the food is authentic and filling. Expect a limited but tasty selection of dishes.

- **Price range:** PKR 800 to PKR 1,200 per person
- **Location:** Multiple branches, including on Hussain Agahi Road
- **Highlights:** Homestyle cooking, fresh roti, and a simple dessert like sheer khurma.
- **Best for:** Those who want a quick, affordable meal without the hotel markup.

## 6. Falettis Hotel Multan

Falettis is another hotel option that offers a decent iftar buffet. It's slightly less expensive than PC or Ramada but still provides a good variety. The buffet includes both desi and Chinese dishes, which is a nice change.

- **Price range:** PKR 1,800 to PKR 2,500 per person
- **Location:** Sher Shah Road
- **Highlights:** Chinese corner with noodles and sweet and sour chicken, plus a live pasta station.
- **Family-friendly:** Yes, with a play area for children.

## How to Choose the Right Iftar Buffet

When picking an iftar buffet in Multan, consider these factors:

- **Budget:** Decide how much you want to spend per person. Prices range from PKR 800 to PKR 3,500.
- **Cuisine:** Do you prefer traditional Pakistani, BBQ, or a mix? Hotels often have more variety.
- **Ambiance:** For a special occasion, a hotel buffet offers a nicer setting. For a quick meal, a restaurant is fine.
- **Location:** Choose a place near your home or work to avoid traffic during Iftar.
- **Reservations:** During Ramadan, buffets fill up fast. Call a day ahead to book a table, especially for large groups.

## Practical Tips for Enjoying Iftar Buffets

- Arrive 15-20 minutes before Iftar time to get a good table and avoid the rush.
- Start with dates and water to break your fast, then move to soups and salads before heavier items.
- Don't overfill on fried snacks; save room for the main course and desserts.
- Check if the buffet includes drinks or if they're extra. Some places charge separately for bottled water or juices.
- If you have dietary restrictions, call ahead to ask about vegetarian or halal options (most are halal, but confirm).

## FAQ

**Q: Are reservations necessary for iftar buffets in Multan?**  
A: Yes, especially at hotels like PC and Ramada. They often have limited seating and high demand during Ramadan. Call at least a day in advance.

**Q: What is the typical iftar buffet price in Multan?**  
A: Prices range from PKR 800 at budget spots to PKR 3,500 at luxury hotels. Most mid-range options cost between PKR 1,500 and PKR 2,500.

**Q: Do these buffets offer separate family sections?**  
A: Many do, including The Nishat Hotel and Bundu Khan. It's best to ask when booking.

**Q: Can I find vegetarian options at these buffets?**  
A: Yes, most buffets include vegetarian dishes like chana chaat, vegetable pakoras, and fruit chaat. Hotels usually have a wider variety.

## The Bottom Line

Multan has an iftar buffet for every taste and budget. For a premium experience, go to Pearl Continental. For good value, try The Nishat Hotel. If you want authentic local food without the frills, Al-Madina is a solid pick. Whatever you choose, book ahead and arrive early to make the most of your iftar. Enjoy your meal and have a blessed Ramadan!
