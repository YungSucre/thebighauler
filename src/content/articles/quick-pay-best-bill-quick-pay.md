Quick pay, also called fast pay or same-day pay, lets you get your freight bill paid in days instead of weeks. For owner-operators and small fleets, this can be the difference between making payroll and missing a payment. The best quick pay service for you depends on your factoring company, your volume, and how fast you need the cash. Here are the top options in 2026, with real numbers and what to watch for.

## What Is Quick Pay and How Does It Work?

Quick pay is an add-on to freight factoring. Instead of waiting 30 to 60 days for the broker to pay, the factoring company advances you the invoice amount, minus a fee, usually within 24 to 48 hours. You still get the rest of the invoice (minus the factoring fee) when the broker pays the factor.

Most factoring companies offer quick pay as a standard feature, but some charge extra. The fee is typically 1% to 3% of the invoice value, or a flat fee per invoice, often $10 to $25. Some factors offer same-day ACH for an additional fee, usually $15 to $30.

## How to Choose the Best Quick Pay for You

Follow these steps to pick the right service:

1. **Check your current factoring agreement.** If you already factor, see if quick pay is included. If not, ask your factor for a quote.
2. **Compare rates and fees.** Get quotes from at least three factors. Look at the factoring rate, any quick pay fees, and hidden charges like application fees or termination fees.
3. **Consider speed.** If you need cash same-day, choose a factor that offers same-day ACH. If 24 hours is fine, you may save money.
4. **Read the fine print.** Some factors require a minimum volume or charge extra for fuel advances. Make sure you understand the terms.
5. **Test with one invoice.** Before signing a long contract, run one invoice through the factor to see how it works.

## What to Watch Out For

Quick pay can be a lifesaver, but it has pitfalls:

- **High fees:** Some factors charge 3% or more per week, which can eat into your profit. Always ask for the effective annual rate.
- **Recourse vs. non-recourse:** Most quick pay is recourse, meaning if the broker doesn't pay, you owe the factor back. Make sure you know which type you have.
- **Hidden charges:** Watch for application fees, monthly minimums, or termination fees. These can add up.
- **Broker credit:** Factors check the broker's credit, not yours. If the broker has poor credit, your invoice may be rejected.

## FAQ

### How fast can I get paid with quick pay?

Most factors fund within 24 hours, and some offer same-day funding if you submit before a cutoff time (usually 11 AM to 1 PM). Same-day funding may cost an extra $15 to $30.

### Is quick pay the same as factoring?

No. Factoring is selling your invoices to a third party. Quick pay is a feature of factoring that speeds up the advance. You still pay the factoring fee, but you get your money faster.

### Can I use quick pay if I have bad credit?

Yes, because factors check the broker's credit, not yours. Your credit score is less important, but factors may look at your business history and the quality of your invoices.

### Are there alternatives to quick pay?

Yes. You can use a fuel card with a cash discount, negotiate faster payment terms with brokers, or use a business line of credit. However, quick pay is often the fastest and easiest option for truckers.
