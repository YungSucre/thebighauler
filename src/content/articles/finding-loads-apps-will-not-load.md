If your load board app is stuck on a spinner or throwing errors, you are not alone. Apps fail for a handful of common reasons, and most of them are fixable in minutes. This guide walks you through the concrete steps to get back to searching loads, and what to do when the app is down for everyone.

## Why Load Board Apps Fail

Load board apps like DAT, Truckstop, and Uber Freight depend on a stable internet connection and their own servers. Common culprits include:

- **Weak cellular signal**: You are parked in a dead zone or the tower is congested.
- **Outdated app version**: The app has a bug that was fixed in an update.
- **Corrupted cache**: Stored data conflicts with new content.
- **Server outage**: The company's servers are down, often during peak hours.
- **Device storage full**: The app cannot write temporary files.
- **Account or payment issue**: Your subscription lapsed or your credentials are wrong.

## Quick Fixes to Try Right Now

### 1. Check Your Connection

First, verify you actually have internet. Open a simple website like Google in your browser. If it loads, your connection is fine. If not, try:

- Toggle airplane mode on and off to reset the radio.
- Move to a different spot, sometimes 50 feet makes a difference.
- Use a Wi-Fi hotspot from your phone or a dedicated hotspot device.
- If you are near a truck stop, ask if they have free Wi-Fi.

### 2. Force Stop and Restart the App

On Android: Settings > Apps > [App Name] > Force Stop. On iPhone: Swipe up from the bottom and swipe the app away. Then reopen the app. This clears minor glitches.

### 3. Clear the Cache (Android Only)

On Android, go to Settings > Apps > [App Name] > Storage > Clear Cache. This does not log you out. On iPhone, you cannot clear cache per app; you must delete and reinstall the app.

### 4. Update the App

Go to the Google Play Store or Apple App Store and check for updates. Load board apps push updates frequently, often to fix bugs or add features. Running an old version is a common cause of failure.

### 5. Restart Your Device

A simple reboot clears memory and resets network connections. It sounds basic, but it works more often than you would think.

### 6. Check Your Subscription and Login

If the app loads but shows no loads, your subscription may have expired. Check your email for renewal notices. Also, log out and log back in to refresh your session.

## When the App Is Down for Everyone

Sometimes the problem is not on your end. Load board servers go down, especially during high-traffic times like Monday mornings. Here is how to check:

- Visit the company's status page (for example, DAT has a status page at dat.com/status).
- Check social media: Twitter or Facebook pages often post outage alerts.
- Call their support line. If you get a busy signal or long hold, that is a clue.

If the app is down, do not panic. You have alternatives.

## Backup Methods for Finding Loads

Do not rely on one app. Here are other ways to find freight when your primary app fails:

- **Call a dispatcher or broker directly**: If you have a regular broker, call them. They can email you load details.
- **Use a different load board**: If you have subscriptions to multiple boards, switch to another. Many drivers carry both DAT and Truckstop.
- **Use the web version**: Most load boards have a website that works in a browser. Log in on your phone or laptop.
- **Check email alerts**: Many boards send load alerts by email. You can view those and call the broker.
- **Visit a truck stop with a kiosk**: Some truck stops have load board terminals, though they are less common now.
- **Use your phone's browser**: Even if the app is broken, the mobile website often works.

## Preventing Future App Failures

Take these steps to reduce the chance of being stuck without loads:

- **Update apps automatically**: Enable auto-updates in your app store settings.
- **Keep your device storage clean**: Delete unused apps and old files. Aim for at least 10% free space.
- **Invest in a good hotspot**: A dedicated hotspot with a strong antenna can improve signal in remote areas. Expect to pay $50-$150 for a decent device, plus a data plan.
- **Carry a backup device**: Some drivers keep an old phone or tablet with the same apps as a backup.
- **Download offline maps**: Not directly for load boards, but it helps you navigate to a better signal area.

## FAQ

### Q: Why does my load board app keep crashing on startup?

A: This is often due to a corrupted cache or an outdated version. Clear the cache (Android) or delete and reinstall the app (iPhone). Also, make sure your device has enough free storage.

### Q: Can I use a load board app on a laptop without a cellular plan?

A: Yes, you can use the web version on a laptop, but you need an internet connection. Many drivers tether to their phone's hotspot or use a mobile hotspot device.

### Q: What should I do if the app says 'No loads available' but I know there are loads?

A: First, check your filters. You may have set a radius or lane that is too narrow. Then log out and back in. If it still shows nothing, check the server status or call support.

### Q: Are there any free load boards that work well?

A: Some free options exist, like Truckstop's free tier or community boards, but they have limited features. For reliable, up-to-date loads, you usually need a paid subscription, which runs $30-$100 per month depending on the board and features.

## The Bottom Line

When a load board app will not load, start with the basics: check your connection, restart the app, clear the cache, and update. If the app is down for everyone, switch to a backup method like the web version or a different board. Keep your apps updated and your device healthy to minimize downtime. And always have a backup plan, because in trucking, time is money. A few minutes of troubleshooting can save you hours of waiting for the app to come back.

Stay safe out there, and keep rolling.
