Factoring your freight invoices is a fast way to get paid without waiting 30 to 60 days. The best factoring companies for truckers offer low rates, quick funding, and no hidden fees. In this guide, we compare six top providers and give you a clear process to pick the right one for your operation.

## What to Look for in a Factoring Company

Before comparing names, know the key factors that separate good factoring from bad.

- **Advance rate**: The percentage of the invoice you get upfront. Most companies offer 90% to 100%. Higher is better, but watch for higher fees.
- **Discount rate (fee)**: The cost of factoring, usually 1% to 5% of the invoice value per month. Rates drop as your volume grows.
- **Recourse vs. non-recourse**: Recourse means you buy back the invoice if the customer doesn't pay. Non-recourse protects you from non-payment, but it costs more and often excludes disputes.
- **Fuel advances**: Some factors let you draw against future invoices for fuel. Useful if you need cash on the road.
- **Customer support**: Look for 24/7 support and a dedicated account manager. You'll deal with them weekly.
- **Contract terms**: Avoid long-term contracts with cancellation penalties. Month-to-month is ideal.
- **Technology**: A good mobile app or online portal speeds up invoice submission and tracking.

## How to Choose the Right Factoring Company

Follow these steps to make the best decision for your business.

1. **Check your customers' credit**: Factoring companies approve based on your customers' payment history, not your credit. If your customers have good credit, you'll get better rates.
2. **Compare total costs**: Don't just look at the discount rate. Factor in application fees, monthly minimums, and any hidden charges. Ask for a sample contract.
3. **Test customer service**: Call the company's support line before signing. See how long you wait and how helpful they are.
4. **Read the contract carefully**: Look for cancellation fees, recourse clauses, and any requirements like minimum volume.
5. **Start with a small contract**: If possible, factor a few invoices with a new company to see how it works before committing.

## FAQ

**Q: How fast can I get funded?**
A: Most top factoring companies offer same-day funding if you submit invoices before a cutoff time (usually 2-3 PM Eastern). Some can fund within hours. Ask about their cutoff times.

**Q: What happens if my customer doesn't pay?**
A: With recourse factoring, you must buy back the invoice after a set period (often 60-90 days). With non-recourse, the factor absorbs the loss, but only if the customer goes bankrupt, not if they dispute the invoice. Read the terms.

**Q: Are there any hidden fees?**
A: Reputable companies are transparent, but watch for application fees, monthly minimums, wire transfer fees, and early termination fees. Ask for a complete fee schedule before signing.

**Q: Can I factor invoices from all my customers?**
A: Most factors require you to factor all invoices from a specific customer, but some allow selective factoring. Check if you can choose which invoices to factor.
