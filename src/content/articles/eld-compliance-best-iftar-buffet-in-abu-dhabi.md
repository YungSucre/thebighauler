If you're looking for the best iftar buffet in Abu Dhabi this Ramadan, you've got plenty of solid options, but the right pick depends on your budget, location, and whether you want a luxury spread or a no-frills feast. This guide breaks down the top buffets by price, quality, and value, so you can break your fast without breaking your bank.

## What to Look for in an Iftar Buffet

Before diving into the list, keep these factors in mind:

- **Price range**: Buffets run from AED 80 to AED 350 per person in 2026. Expect higher prices at five-star hotels.
- **Cuisine variety**: Most buffets offer Middle Eastern, Indian, Asian, and international dishes. Check if they have live cooking stations.
- **Location**: Traffic around sunset can be brutal. Pick a spot close to your route or accommodation.
- **Booking**: Many popular spots fill up fast. Reserve at least 2-3 days ahead, especially for weekends.
- **Timing**: Iftar usually starts at sunset (around 6:30 PM in Abu Dhabi during Ramadan 2026). Arrive 15-20 minutes early to avoid the rush.

## Top 5 Iftar Buffets in Abu Dhabi

### 1. Emirates Palace Mandarin Oriental

This is the gold standard for iftar in Abu Dhabi. The buffet is massive, with over 200 dishes, including live stations for grilled meats, seafood, and Arabic sweets. You'll find everything from lamb ouzi to fresh sushi.

- **Price**: AED 350-450 per person (including soft drinks).
- **Location**: Corniche Road, near the Presidential Palace.
- **Pros**: Unmatched luxury, huge variety, stunning setting.
- **Cons**: Pricey, and you'll need to book weeks in advance.
- **Best for**: Special occasions or if you want to splurge.

### 2. Fairmont Bab Al Bahr

Located on the Maqta Creek, this buffet offers a mix of Arabic, Indian, and international dishes. The outdoor terrace has views of the Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque, which is a nice touch.

- **Price**: AED 250-320 per person.
- **Location**: Between the city and the airport, easy access from the highway.
- **Pros**: Great views, good variety, family-friendly.
- **Cons**: Can get crowded; booking is essential.
- **Best for**: Families and groups.

### 3. Sofitel Abu Dhabi Corniche

This buffet is known for its generous spread and reasonable price for a five-star. Expect live cooking stations, a dedicated Arabic corner, and a wide dessert selection.

- **Price**: AED 200-260 per person.
- **Location**: Corniche Road, central.
- **Pros**: Value for money, good for couples.
- **Cons**: The buffet line can be long at peak times.
- **Best for**: Those who want quality without the premium price.

### 4. Al Dhafra Beach Hotel

A budget-friendly option that doesn't skimp on taste. The buffet includes traditional Emirati dishes, grilled meats, and a selection of salads and desserts.

- **Price**: AED 120-150 per person.
- **Location**: Near the Corniche, but a bit off the main strip.
- **Pros**: Affordable, decent variety, less crowded.
- **Cons**: Limited international options.
- **Best for**: Solo diners or small budgets.

### 5. Local Restaurants in Khalidiya

If you want a more local experience, many smaller restaurants in Khalidiya offer iftar buffets for AED 80-120. These are often simple but filling, with rice, grilled chicken, and curries.

- **Price**: AED 80-120 per person.
- **Location**: Various spots along Khalidiya Street.
- **Pros**: Cheap, authentic, no reservation needed.
- **Cons**: Basic setting, limited choices.
- **Best for**: Quick, no-frills meals.

## How to Book and Save

- **Book online**: Use apps like Zomato, Talabat, or the hotel's website. Many offer early-bird discounts of 10-15%.
- **Group deals**: If you're with 4 or more, ask about group rates. Some places give a free meal for every 5 paying guests.
- **Check for promotions**: Credit card companies (like ADCB or Emirates NBD) often have Ramadan offers. Look for 2-for-1 deals.
- **Go on weekdays**: Weekends (Thursday and Friday) are pricier and busier. If your schedule allows, go on a Sunday to Wednesday.

## FAQ

### Can I get a takeaway iftar buffet?

Yes, many hotels offer takeaway iftar boxes. Prices range from AED 60 to 150 per box, depending on the spread. Check with the venue ahead of time.

### Are there any free iftar buffets?

Some mosques and community centers offer free iftar meals for those in need, especially in areas like Al Wahda or Musaffah. These are simple meals, not buffets, but they're open to all.

### What time does iftar start in Abu Dhabi?

During Ramadan 2026, sunset is around 6:30 PM in Abu Dhabi. Buffets typically open 30 minutes before and close around 9 PM.

### Do I need to book in advance?

For luxury hotels, yes, at least 2-3 days ahead. For budget places, you can usually walk in, but it's safer to call ahead on weekends.

## The Bottom Line

For the best overall iftar buffet in Abu Dhabi, the Sofitel Corniche gives you the best balance of price, quality, and variety. If you're on a tight budget, the Al Dhafra Beach Hotel is a solid choice. And if it's a special occasion, Emirates Palace is worth the splurge. Book early, check for discounts, and arrive on time to make the most of your iftar experience.
