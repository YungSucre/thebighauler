If you're a truck driver, owner-operator, or small fleet owner, staying compliant with DOT regulations is non-negotiable. But you don't have to do it alone. The best DOT compliance companies handle the paperwork, tracking, and filing so you can focus on driving. Here's a practical look at the top providers, what they cost, and how to choose one that fits your operation.

## What DOT Compliance Companies Do

DOT compliance companies manage the administrative side of federal and state regulations. They typically offer:

- **ELD compliance**: Ensure your electronic logging device meets FMCSA standards and data is transferred correctly.
- **Hours of Service (HOS) management**: Track driver hours, flag violations, and help with logs.
- **IFTA filing**: Calculate and submit quarterly fuel tax reports.
- **Drug and alcohol testing**: Random testing pools, consortium management, and reporting.
- **Permits and registrations**: Handle IRP, UCR, and other state permits.
- **Audit support**: Prepare you for DOT audits and represent you in case of violations.

Some companies offer full-service compliance, while others focus on specific areas. The best choice depends on your needs and budget.

## Top DOT Compliance Companies in 2026

Here are the leading providers, based on reputation, features, and pricing. Prices are monthly per truck unless noted.

### 1. J.J. Keller & Associates

J.J. Keller is a giant in compliance training and services. They offer a comprehensive compliance program that includes ELD (via their Encompass platform), HOS, IFTA, and drug testing management.

- **Best for**: Fleets that want a one-stop shop with a trusted name.
- **Pricing**: $40-$80 per truck/month, depending on services. Training courses are extra.
- **Pros**: Extensive resources, excellent customer support, strong reputation.
- **Cons**: Can be pricey for small fleets; some features may be overkill.

### 2. Foley Services

Foley is known for its IFTA and fuel tax filing, but they also offer permits, ELD compliance, and safety programs.

- **Best for**: Owner-operators and small fleets focused on IFTA and permits.
- **Pricing**: IFTA filing starts around $25 per quarter; full compliance packages $30-$60 per truck/month.
- **Pros**: Affordable, specialized in fuel tax, easy online portal.
- **Cons**: Less robust for large fleets; drug testing not a core focus.

### 3. Trucking Office

Trucking Office provides a cloud-based platform that combines compliance, dispatch, and accounting. It's designed for small to mid-sized fleets.

- **Best for**: Fleets that want an all-in-one back-office solution.
- **Pricing**: $50-$100 per truck/month, depending on modules.
- **Pros**: Integrates compliance with dispatch and billing, user-friendly.
- **Cons**: More expensive than basic compliance-only services.

### 4. EROAD

EROAD is primarily an ELD provider, but they also offer HOS, IFTA, and fuel tax reporting. Their hardware is reliable and easy to install.

- **Best for**: Fleets that need a solid ELD with compliance add-ons.
- **Pricing**: ELD starts at $25-$35 per truck/month; IFTA add-on around $10/month.
- **Pros**: Strong ELD hardware, good mobile app, transparent pricing.
- **Cons**: Limited drug testing and permit services.

### 5. Motive (formerly KeepTruckin)

Motive is a popular ELD and fleet management platform. It includes HOS, IFTA, and basic compliance features.

- **Best for**: Small fleets and owner-operators looking for an affordable ELD.
- **Pricing**: ELD starts at $20-$30 per truck/month; IFTA and compliance add-ons extra.
- **Pros**: Easy to use, good customer support, scalable.
- **Cons**: Compliance services are not as comprehensive as full-service providers.

### 6. Compliance Safety Consulting (CSC)

CSC offers hands-on compliance services, including safety audits, policy manuals, and driver qualification file management.

- **Best for**: Fleets that need personalized, consulting-style support.
- **Pricing**: Custom quotes; typical $100-$200 per truck/month for full service.
- **Pros**: Expert consultants, thorough audits, great for high-risk fleets.
- **Cons**: Higher cost, not a software platform.

## How to Choose the Right Provider

Follow these steps to pick the best DOT compliance company for your situation:

1. **Assess your needs**: List the compliance areas you struggle with. If you only need IFTA, a specialized provider like Foley might be enough. If you need everything, go with J.J. Keller or Trucking Office.
2. **Set a budget**: Know what you can spend per truck each month. Remember, non-compliance fines can cost hundreds or thousands, so investing in compliance is worth it.
3. **Check reviews and references**: Look for reviews from other truckers. Ask the provider for references from fleets similar to yours.
4. **Test the software**: Most providers offer a free trial or demo. Try it for a week to see if it's user-friendly.
5. **Verify FMCSA compliance**: Ensure the provider's ELD is registered with FMCSA and their services meet current regulations.

## FAQ

**Q: How much do DOT compliance services cost?**
A: Prices vary widely. Basic ELD plans start around $20-$30 per truck/month. Full-service compliance packages range from $40-$100 per truck/month. Consulting services can be $100-$200 per truck/month.

**Q: Can I handle DOT compliance myself?**
A: Yes, but it's time-consuming and error-prone. You must track HOS, file IFTA quarterly, maintain driver qualification files, and manage drug testing. Many small fleets find it worth the cost to outsource.

**Q: What happens if I don't comply with DOT regulations?**
A: You can face fines, out-of-service orders, and even loss of your CDL. For example, HOS violations can cost up to $16,000 per offense. Compliance companies help you avoid these penalties.

**Q: Do these companies help with DOT audits?**
A: Yes, most offer audit support. They can review your files, prepare you for the audit, and represent you if needed. This is especially valuable if you're at risk of a compliance review.

## The Bottom Line

The best DOT compliance company for you depends on your fleet size, budget, and specific needs. For most small fleets, Motive or EROAD offer affordable ELD solutions with basic compliance features. If you need comprehensive services, J.J. Keller or Trucking Office are solid choices. For specialized IFTA help, Foley is hard to beat. Take advantage of free trials, compare pricing, and choose a provider that will keep you legal and on the road. Remember, compliance isn't just about avoiding fines; it's about safety and professionalism. Invest in a good compliance partner and you'll have peace of mind on every mile.
