Finding the best iftar buffet in Rawalpindi in 2026 means balancing variety, quality, and value. Whether you're a local or just passing through, these top spots offer solid spreads for breaking your fast. Here’s a direct rundown of the best options, with realistic prices and what to expect.

## Top Iftar Buffets in Rawalpindi

### 1. Pearl Continental (PC) Rawalpindi

**Price Range:** Rs. 3,500 to Rs. 4,500 per person (2026)

PC is the gold standard for iftar buffets in Rawalpindi. The spread includes live grills, a massive variety of Pakistani and continental dishes, and a dedicated dessert section with traditional sweets like jalebi and gulab jamun. The ambiance is upscale, and service is attentive. Expect a bustling crowd, so book ahead, especially on weekends.

**What You Get:**
- Live BBQ counter with seekh kebabs, chicken tikka, and malai boti
- Extensive salad bar and chaat corner
- Traditional drinks like dahi bhalla and fruit chaat
- Indoor seating with air conditioning

**Practical Tip:** Reserve a table at least 3 days in advance for the last 10 days of Ramadan. Valet parking is available but fills up fast.

### 2. Monal Restaurant (Pir Sohawa)

**Price Range:** Rs. 2,800 to Rs. 3,500 per person

Located on the Margalla Hills, Monal offers a stunning view of Rawalpindi and Islamabad. The iftar buffet is generous, with a mix of local and continental dishes. The outdoor terrace is popular for the sunset view, but it gets cold in the evening, so bring a jacket.

**What You Get:**
- Wide variety of BBQ and traditional Pakistani curries
- Live pasta and pizza station for kids
- Fresh fruit and dessert counter
- Scenic drive up the hill, but traffic can be heavy

**Practical Tip:** Arrive at least 45 minutes before iftar to secure a good spot and enjoy the sunset. The road is winding; drive carefully.

### 3. Savour Foods (Multiple Branches)

**Price Range:** Rs. 1,500 to Rs. 2,000 per person

Savour Foods is a popular family-friendly chain known for its consistent quality. Their iftar buffet includes a mix of traditional items like pakoras, samosas, and a variety of main courses. It’s a budget-friendly option without compromising on taste.

**What You Get:**
- Standard Pakistani iftar items: pakoras, samosas, chana chaat
- Rice dishes, karahi, and naan
- Desserts like kheer and ice cream
- Casual, family-oriented atmosphere

**Practical Tip:** Branch locations vary; the one on Murree Road is central. Expect long queues near iftar time, so go early or order takeaway.

### 4. Lal Qila Restaurant

**Price Range:** Rs. 2,500 to Rs. 3,000 per person

Lal Qila offers a traditional Mughlai-themed dining experience. The iftar buffet features a rich array of grilled meats, biryani, and classic curries. The decor is ornate, giving a royal feel, and it’s a good choice for those who want a cultural experience.

**What You Get:**
- Extensive BBQ selection: mutton chops, chicken boti, fish tikka
- Handi and karahi dishes cooked in desi ghee
- Live tandoor for fresh naan and roti
- Indoor seating with traditional seating options (floor seating available)

**Practical Tip:** The floor seating (takht) is popular; request it when booking. Service can be slow during peak hours, so be patient.

### 5. Khiva Restaurant

**Price Range:** Rs. 2,200 to Rs. 2,800 per person

Khiva is a newer entrant but has quickly gained a reputation for its elegant ambiance and diverse buffet. It offers a mix of Pakistani, Middle Eastern, and continental dishes, making it a great choice for groups with varied tastes.

**What You Get:**
- Middle Eastern mezze and grills
- Pakistani classics like nihari and haleem
- Live omelette station for suhoor (if they offer it, but for iftar, it’s a plus)
- Modern, chic interior

**Practical Tip:** They often have special family deals on weekdays. Check their social media for discounts.

### 6. Salt’n Pepper Village

**Price Range:** Rs. 2,000 to Rs. 2,500 per person

This restaurant is known for its rustic village-style decor and hearty Pakistani food. The iftar buffet is straightforward but satisfying, with a focus on traditional flavors. It’s a good option for those who prefer a no-frills experience.

**What You Get:**
- Classic BBQ and karahi items
- Lentil dishes and vegetable sides
- Fresh chutneys and pickles
- Outdoor seating available in a courtyard setting

**Practical Tip:** The outdoor seating is nice but can be hot; choose indoor if you prefer air conditioning.

## How to Choose the Right Buffet

- **Budget:** If you’re on a tight budget, Savour Foods offers the best value. For a splurge, PC is worth it.
- **Ambiance:** For a romantic iftar, Monal’s view is unbeatable. For a cultural feel, Lal Qila.
- **Group Size:** Large groups may prefer Khiva or Salt’n Pepper Village for their spacious seating.
- **Food Variety:** PC and Khiva offer the widest range of cuisines.

## Practical Tips for Iftar Buffets

- **Book in Advance:** Popular spots fill up, especially on weekends. Call at least 2 days ahead.
- **Arrive Early:** Reach 30 minutes before iftar to avoid the rush and get a good table.
- **Check for Deals:** Many restaurants offer discounts for children or group bookings. Ask when reserving.
- **Consider Takeaway:** If you prefer to eat at home, some places offer iftar boxes. Check with the restaurant.

## FAQ

### 1. Are reservations necessary for iftar buffets in Rawalpindi?
Yes, especially at high-end places like PC and Monal. Walk-ins are possible but may face long waits or no availability.

### 2. What is the average cost of an iftar buffet in Rawalpindi in 2026?
Prices range from Rs. 1,500 to Rs. 4,500 per person, depending on the restaurant and menu.

### 3. Do these buffets offer separate family sections?
Most family-friendly restaurants like Savour Foods and Lal Qila have family seating areas. PC and Khiva also have private dining options.

### 4. Can I get a discount for large groups?
Many restaurants offer group discounts for 10 or more. It’s best to call and negotiate directly.

## The Bottom Line

The best iftar buffet in Rawalpindi for 2026 depends on your budget and preference. For a premium experience, Pearl Continental is the top choice. For a scenic view, Monal is unmatched. If you want value, Savour Foods delivers. Whichever you pick, book ahead and arrive early to make the most of your iftar. Enjoy your meal and have a blessed Ramadan.
