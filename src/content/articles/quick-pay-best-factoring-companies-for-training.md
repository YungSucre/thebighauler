If you're new to trucking and need cash flow fast, factoring your freight invoices is a solid move. But not all factoring companies are the same, especially when it comes to training. Some offer real education on back-office tasks, compliance, and growth. This guide breaks down the best factoring companies for training, with realistic rates and what you get for your money.

## What to Look for in a Factoring Company for Training

Training support can mean different things. Look for these features:

- **Onboarding tutorials**: Video or written guides on submitting invoices, using the dashboard, and understanding fees.
- **Ongoing webinars**: Monthly or quarterly sessions on topics like ELD compliance, IFTA filing, or load planning.
- **Dedicated account manager**: A human who answers questions and helps you learn the ropes.
- **Resource library**: Articles, checklists, and templates for things like rate negotiation or fuel tax.
- **Free vs. paid**: Some companies bundle training with their service; others charge extra.

## Top Factoring Companies with Strong Training Programs

Here are five factoring companies known for their training support, based on driver reviews and industry reputation as of early 2026.

### 1. Triumph Business Capital

Triumph is a big name in factoring, and they offer a robust onboarding process. New clients get a dedicated account executive who walks you through the portal and explains how to submit invoices. They also have a resource center with articles on cash flow, fuel management, and compliance. Rates typically range from 0.5% to 3% per month, depending on volume and creditworthiness. Their training is free and included.

### 2. RTS Financial

RTS Financial is known for its educational approach. They offer a "Learning Center" with webinars and guides on topics like managing receivables and understanding DOT rules. Their onboarding includes a step-by-step setup and a personal representative. Rates are competitive, often 0.5% to 2.5% per month. They also have a mobile app that makes it easy to learn on the go.

### 3. Factor Finders

Factor Finders is a smaller company that prides itself on personalized service. They provide one-on-one training during setup, and their account managers are available for ongoing questions. They also publish a blog with practical tips for owner-operators. Rates are a bit higher, typically 1% to 4% per month, but the training is hands-on and tailored to your needs.

### 4. BlueTiger Funding

BlueTiger focuses on small fleets and offers a simple, transparent fee structure. Their training includes a comprehensive onboarding packet and access to a support team that explains everything from invoice submission to fee calculations. They also host quarterly webinars on industry trends. Rates range from 0.5% to 2% per month, with no hidden fees.

### 5. TBS Factoring

TBS is another established player with a strong training component. They offer a "Client Success Team" that provides ongoing education, including how to avoid bad debt and manage cash flow. Their online portal has a library of tutorials. Rates are typically 0.5% to 3% per month. They also offer fuel advances and other services that can help new drivers.

## How to Evaluate Training Quality Before Signing Up

Don't just take their word for it. Do these steps:

1. **Ask for a demo**: Most companies will walk you through their portal. Pay attention to how they explain the process.
2. **Check reviews**: Look on forums like TruckersReport or Reddit. See what current clients say about training.
3. **Request a sample training session**: Some companies will let you preview a webinar or guide.
4. **Ask about ongoing support**: Find out if training is one-time or continuous. You want a partner that grows with you.

## FAQ

### Do factoring companies charge extra for training?

Most include basic training for free as part of their service. Some might charge for advanced courses, but that's rare. Always clarify upfront.

### Can I use factoring if I have bad credit?

Yes. Factoring is based on your customer's credit, not yours. However, your credit may affect the rate or advance percentage. Some companies are more lenient than others.

### How fast can I get funded?

Typically within 24 hours after you submit an invoice. Some companies offer same-day funding for an extra fee.

### What happens if my customer doesn't pay?

If you have non-recourse factoring, the company absorbs the loss. Recourse factoring means you buy back the invoice. Training often covers how to assess customer credit to minimize this risk.
