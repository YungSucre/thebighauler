If you need a hazmat endorsement (HME) or annual hazmat refresher training, online courses are a practical, cost-effective way to get it done. The best options range from $25 to $200 per course, with most offering the required PHMSA-compliant curriculum and a certificate you can use immediately. Below, we break down the top online hazmat training providers, what they cost, and how to choose the right one for your situation.

## What to Look for in an Online Hazmat Course

Before you pay for any course, verify these three things:

- **PHMSA compliance**: The course must cover 49 CFR parts 171-180, including general awareness, function-specific, safety, and security awareness training. The certificate should state this.
- **Certificate validity**: Your employer or the DOT may require a specific format. Most courses provide a PDF certificate with your name, course date, and expiration (typically 3 years for initial, 1 year for refresher).
- **State acceptance**: Some states (like California) have additional requirements for hazmat endorsements. Check with your state's DMV or your employer before enrolling.

## Top Online Hazmat Training Courses (2026)

Here are the most reputable providers, based on industry reputation, course depth, and price. Prices are per student and reflect current ranges as of early 2026.

- **Pros**: Expert instructors, real-world examples, and a strong reputation in the industry.
- **Cons**: Higher price point; the course is more detailed than what many drivers need.

### 4. 360training (Hazmat Endorsement Training)

## How to Choose the Right Course for You

Follow these steps to pick the best course for your situation:

1. **Check your employer's requirements**: Some companies have a list of approved providers. Ask your safety manager first.
2. **Identify your training need**: Are you getting a new HME, or just refreshing? Initial training requires a full course (3-year cycle), while refresher is shorter (1-year cycle).
3. **Verify state DMV rules**: If you're taking the HME test, confirm that your state accepts online training as preparation. Most do, but some require in-person verification.
4. **Read reviews**: Look for recent reviews from other drivers. Check forums like TruckersReport for real-world feedback.
5. **Buy the course**: Once you choose, complete it within a week. Most courses are self-paced and take 2 to 8 hours.

## FAQ

### Is online hazmat training accepted by the DOT?

Yes, the DOT allows online training for hazmat employees, as long as the course covers the required topics and you receive a certificate. The training must be documented and kept on file.

### How often do I need hazmat refresher training?

You need refresher training every 3 years for general awareness, function-specific, and safety training. However, many employers require annual security awareness training, so check your company policy.

### Can I take the hazmat endorsement test online?

No, the HME written test must be taken in person at your state's DMV or testing center. Online courses prepare you for the test, but you must schedule and pass the official exam.

### What happens if I fail the online course exam?

Most providers allow unlimited retakes until you pass. Some charge a small fee for a second attempt, so read the fine print before buying.
