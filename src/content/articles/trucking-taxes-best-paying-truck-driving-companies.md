If you're looking for the best paying truck driving companies, you need to know where the money is and what it takes to get hired. Pay varies widely by experience, type of freight, and region, but some companies consistently offer above-average rates. This guide breaks down the top-paying carriers, realistic pay ranges, and steps you can take this week to position yourself for a high-paying job.

## What Defines a High-Paying Trucking Company?
High pay isn't just about the per-mile rate. It's about total compensation: base pay, bonuses, benefits, and home time. A company paying 60 cents per mile but keeping you out for three weeks might pay less than one paying 50 cents with weekly home time. Also consider paid detention, layover, and stop pay, which can add $5,000 to $10,000 annually.

## Top Paying Truck Driving Companies in 2026
Here are the companies that consistently rank among the best for pay, based on industry data and driver reviews. Figures are realistic ranges for experienced drivers (2+ years) in 2026.

### 1. Walmart Transportation
Walmart is known for top-tier pay and benefits. Drivers earn $0.95 to $1.10 per mile, with average annual earnings between $100,000 and $120,000. They offer a 401(k) match, profit sharing, and generous home time. Requirements: 30 months of verifiable experience, no more than two moving violations in the last three years, and a clean safety record.

### 2. UPS Freight (TForce)
UPS Freight pays hourly, with experienced drivers earning $35 to $42 per hour. Annual earnings range from $90,000 to $110,000, including overtime. They offer strong benefits and union representation. Requirements: 2 years of experience, a clean record, and ability to pass a rigorous background check.

### 3. Old Dominion Freight Line
Old Dominion is a less-than-truckload (LTL) carrier that pays well. Linehaul drivers earn $0.70 to $0.85 per mile, with annual earnings of $85,000 to $100,000. They offer excellent benefits and a performance bonus. Requirements: 2 years of experience, good driving record, and willingness to work nights and weekends.

### 4. Sysco
Sysco is a foodservice distributor that pays drivers by the hour or by the case. Experienced drivers earn $30 to $38 per hour, with annual earnings of $85,000 to $100,000. The work is physically demanding, but pay is steady. Requirements: 1 year of experience, ability to lift 50-70 pounds, and a clean record.

### 5. Schneider (Dedicated and Tanker)
Schneider offers dedicated and tanker positions that pay above average. Experienced drivers earn $0.60 to $0.80 per mile, with annual earnings of $75,000 to $95,000. They offer paid training for tanker endorsement. Requirements: 1 year of experience for dedicated, 2 years for tanker, and a clean record.

### 6. Prime Inc. (Tanker and Team)
Prime Inc. is known for high-paying tanker and team driving positions. Tanker drivers earn $0.65 to $0.85 per mile, and team drivers can split $0.90 to $1.10 per mile combined. Annual earnings range from $80,000 to $100,000 for solo tanker, and $100,000 to $130,000 per driver for teams. Requirements: 1 year of experience for tanker, 2 years for team, and a clean record.

## How to Get Hired by a High-Paying Company
Getting a job with a top-paying carrier requires more than a CDL. Here are practical steps you can take this week:

1. **Clean up your MVR**: Order your motor vehicle record from your state DMV. Fix any errors and avoid new violations. Most top companies require no more than one or two moving violations in the last three years.
2. **Get endorsements**: Tanker, hazmat, and doubles/triples endorsements can boost your pay by $0.10 to $0.20 per mile. Study for the written tests and schedule them at your local DMV.
3. **Build experience**: If you have less than two years, consider a regional or dedicated job that offers consistent miles. Many top companies require 2+ years, so plan accordingly.
4. **Improve your safety record**: Take a defensive driving course. Some companies offer discounts on insurance, and it shows commitment to safety.
5. **Apply strategically**: Don't just apply online. Call recruiters, ask about current pay and home time, and negotiate. Use your experience and endorsements as leverage.

## FAQ
### What is the highest paying truck driving job?
The highest paying jobs are often in specialized fields like tanker, hazmat, or team driving. Walmart and UPS Freight are among the top-paying companies, with annual earnings exceeding $100,000 for experienced drivers.

### How much do truck drivers make per mile in 2026?
Average per-mile rates range from $0.50 to $0.70 for experienced drivers, but top companies pay $0.80 to $1.10 per mile. Rates vary by region, freight type, and experience.

### Do I need a college degree to get a high-paying trucking job?
No. A CDL and a clean driving record are the main requirements. Endorsements and experience are more important than a degree.

### How can I increase my trucking pay?
Get endorsements, gain experience, consider specialized freight like tanker or hazmat, and negotiate your pay. Also, look for companies that offer bonuses for safety, fuel efficiency, and retention.

## The Bottom Line
The best paying truck driving companies offer more than just a high per-mile rate. They provide consistent miles, good home time, and benefits that add up. Walmart, UPS Freight, and Old Dominion are top choices, but they require experience and a clean record. If you're new, focus on building experience and getting endorsements. If you're experienced, don't settle for average pay: apply to the best, negotiate, and leverage your skills. The money is there if you know where to look and how to position yourself.
