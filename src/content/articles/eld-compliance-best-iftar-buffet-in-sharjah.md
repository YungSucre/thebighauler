Finding the best iftar buffet in Sharjah in 2026 means balancing variety, quality, and value. Whether you're a resident or just visiting during Ramadan, this guide covers the top spots with realistic price ranges and what to expect. From luxurious hotel spreads to local favorites, you'll know exactly where to go for a satisfying break-fast.

## Al Bustan Centre & Residence

Al Bustan Centre offers a popular iftar buffet that combines Middle Eastern and international dishes. Expect a spread of grilled meats, traditional Arabic mezze, live cooking stations, and a dessert table with Umm Ali and kunafa. The buffet runs daily from sunset to around 9:30 PM.

Price range: AED 120 to AED 150 per person. Children under 6 eat free, and kids aged 6 to 12 get a 50% discount. During the last 10 days of Ramadan, prices may increase slightly, so book early.

## DoubleTree by Hilton Sharjah

Located on the Corniche, the DoubleTree offers a waterfront iftar buffet with stunning views. The menu features Arabic specialties, seafood, and a dedicated section for international cuisine. They also have a live Oud music performance, adding to the ambiance.

Price range: AED 149 to AED 179 per person. Beverages like Ramadan juices and Arabic coffee are included. For families, there's a kids' corner with activities, making it a good choice for those with children.

## Radisson Blu Resort, Sharjah

This resort's iftar buffet is known for its extensive variety, including a sushi station, Indian curries, and a live grill. The setting is elegant, with both indoor and outdoor seating overlooking the pool. They also offer a special "Iftar at the Beach" option for a more casual experience.

Price range: AED 169 to AED 199 per person. The price includes soft drinks and traditional Ramadan drinks. For an extra AED 50, you can add a shisha corner experience.

## Al Rayan Restaurant at Centro Sharjah

For a budget-friendly yet satisfying iftar, Al Rayan Restaurant at Centro Sharjah offers a no-frills buffet with a focus on Arabic and Asian dishes. It's a popular spot among locals and expats for its value and quality. The buffet includes soups, salads, grilled meats, and a variety of desserts.

Price range: AED 89 to AED 109 per person. This is one of the most affordable options in Sharjah, making it ideal for large groups or those on a tight budget.

## Arabian Courtyard Hotel & Spa

This hotel offers a traditional iftar buffet in a courtyard setting, with a menu that emphasizes Emirati and Middle Eastern cuisine. Highlights include lamb ouzi, machboos, and a range of Arabic sweets. The atmosphere is calm and family-friendly.

Price range: AED 139 to AED 159 per person. They offer a 20% discount for groups of 10 or more, which is great for community iftars.

## Tips for Choosing the Best Iftar Buffet

When selecting an iftar buffet, consider these practical factors:

- **Location**: Choose a spot close to your home or workplace to avoid traffic during peak hours. Sharjah Corniche and major hotels are convenient.
- **Menu Variety**: Check if the buffet includes your preferred cuisines. Some places specialize in Arabic, while others offer international options.
- **Ambiance**: If you prefer a quiet setting, opt for hotels with separate family sections. For a lively atmosphere, choose places with live music.
- **Price vs. Value**: Compare what's included. Some buffets include beverages and shisha, which can add value.
- **Reservations**: During Ramadan, especially on weekends, restaurants fill up fast. Book at least 2-3 days in advance.
- **Special Offers**: Look for early bird discounts or group deals on hotel websites or dining apps.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: What time does iftar buffet typically start in Sharjah?**
A: Iftar buffets start at sunset, which varies each day during Ramadan. In 2026, sunset in Sharjah is around 6:30 PM to 7:00 PM. Restaurants usually open the buffet 15 minutes before sunset and serve until about 9:30 PM.

**Q: Are there any vegetarian options at these buffets?**
A: Yes, most iftar buffets in Sharjah offer a variety of vegetarian dishes, including falafel, hummus, salads, and vegetable curries. Some places also have live cooking stations where you can customize your meal.

**Q: Can I book an iftar buffet for a large group?**
A: Absolutely. Most hotels and restaurants accommodate large groups, but you need to book in advance. Some offer special group rates, like the Arabian Courtyard's 20% discount for 10 or more people.

**Q: Do these buffets include drinks?**
A: Most buffets include water, Ramadan juices (like Jallab and Vimto), and Arabic coffee. Soft drinks and other beverages may be extra, so check with the venue.

## The Bottom Line

The best iftar buffet in Sharjah depends on your budget and preferences. For a premium experience with a view, the DoubleTree by Hilton or Radisson Blu are excellent choices. If you want a great meal without breaking the bank, Al Rayan Restaurant offers solid value. Book early, especially for the last 10 days of Ramadan, and consider location and menu variety to make your iftar memorable. Enjoy your evening!
