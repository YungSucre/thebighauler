If you're an owner-operator or small fleet owner looking for a free dispatch tool, the best options are TruckLogics, Rose Rocket (free tier), and SuperTruckers. These tools handle load assignment, driver communication, and basic tracking without a monthly fee. But free tiers come with limits: fewer loads, fewer drivers, or reduced reporting. This guide breaks down the top free dispatch tools, what they actually include, and how to pick one that fits your operation.

## What to Look for in a Free Dispatch Tool

Before downloading anything, know what you need. A dispatch tool should do three things: assign loads, track status, and communicate with drivers. Free versions often cap these features. Here's what to check:

- **Load limits**: Most free plans cap active loads at 5 to 10 per month. If you run more, you'll need a paid plan.
- **Driver limits**: Free tiers usually allow 1 to 3 drivers. If you have a larger fleet, look elsewhere.
- **Tracking**: GPS tracking is often limited to a few vehicles or requires a paid add-on.
- **IFTA and fuel logging**: Many free tools skip these, so you'll need separate software.
- **Customer support**: Free plans usually have email-only support, with response times of 24 to 48 hours.

## Detailed Reviews of the Top Picks

### TruckLogics

TruckLogics is a solid choice for owner-operators. The free plan includes dispatch, invoicing, and basic IFTA tracking. You can assign loads to one driver, update statuses, and send invoices. The catch: you're limited to 5 active loads per month, and GPS tracking is not included. If you need more, the paid plan starts at $35 per month per user, which adds unlimited loads and GPS.

**Pros**: Simple interface, includes invoicing, IFTA tracking on free plan.
**Cons**: 5-load limit, no GPS on free tier.

### Rose Rocket

Rose Rocket is a full TMS with a free tier for small fleets. You get 10 loads, 3 drivers, and basic dispatch features like load assignment and status updates. The free plan also includes customer portal access, which is rare. However, you'll need to pay for GPS tracking and advanced reporting. Paid plans start at $100 per month per user, which might be steep for a small operation.

**Pros**: Generous free limits, customer portal, good for growing fleets.
**Cons**: Paid plans are pricey, GPS is not free.

### SuperTruckers

SuperTruckers combines a dispatch tool with a load board. The free plan gives you 10 loads, 2 drivers, and access to their load board. You can post loads, assign them, and track status. The downside: no IFTA or fuel logging, and customer support is limited. Paid plans start at $30 per month and add features like document management and advanced tracking.

**Pros**: Load board included, free plan is generous.
**Cons**: No IFTA, support is slow.

### Axon Software

Axon Software is a lesser-known option but works well for owner-operators. The free plan includes 5 loads, 1 driver, and basic dispatch. You get load assignment, status updates, and simple reporting. However, there's no GPS tracking, and the interface feels dated. Paid plans start at $50 per month and add GPS and more loads.

**Pros**: Simple, no learning curve.
**Cons**: Dated interface, no GPS on free tier.

### Logity Dispatch

Logity Dispatch is a lightweight tool for solo drivers. The free plan allows 3 loads and 1 driver. You can assign loads and track status, but that's about it. No invoicing, no IFTA. Paid plans start at $25 per month, which is cheap, but you might outgrow it quickly.

**Pros**: Low cost, easy to use.
**Cons**: Very limited free plan, no extra features.

### Truckstop TMS

Truckstop TMS is part of the Truckstop load board ecosystem. The free plan includes 10 loads, 2 drivers, and basic dispatch. You also get access to their load board, which is a plus. However, the free plan lacks GPS and invoicing. Paid plans start at $75 per month and include those features.

**Pros**: Load board integration, decent free limits.
**Cons**: Paid plans are pricey, no invoicing on free tier.

## How to Choose the Right Tool for Your Operation

Follow these steps to pick the best free dispatch tool for your needs:

1. **List your must-haves**: Write down the features you can't live without: GPS, IFTA, invoicing, load board. If a free plan lacks them, you'll need to pay or find a workaround.
2. **Count your loads and drivers**: If you run more than 10 loads a month or have more than 2 drivers, a free plan won't cut it. Look for a paid plan that fits your volume.
3. **Test the free plan first**: Sign up for 2 or 3 free plans and use them for a week. See which one feels natural. You'll be using it daily, so ease of use matters.
4. **Check integration with your other tools**: If you use QuickBooks or a specific ELD, make sure the dispatch tool integrates. Otherwise, you'll waste time on double entry.
5. **Read the fine print on data limits**: Some free plans limit data storage or number of customers. Know the limits before you commit.

## FAQ

### Can I really run my dispatch for free?

Yes, if you're an owner-operator with 5 to 10 loads a month and one driver. But you'll likely need to supplement with separate tools for GPS, IFTA, or invoicing. For a small fleet, free plans are often too limited, and you'll end up paying $50-$150 per month for a full TMS.

### What's the difference between a dispatch tool and a TMS?

A dispatch tool focuses on load assignment and tracking. A TMS (Transportation Management System) includes dispatch plus accounting, IFTA, fuel logs, and customer management. Free dispatch tools are usually stripped-down TMSs.

### Are free dispatch tools safe for my data?

Most reputable tools use encryption and follow industry standards. But free plans may have less robust security. Always use strong passwords and enable two-factor authentication if available.

### Will free tools work with my ELD?

Some do, like TruckLogics and Rose Rocket, but not all. Check the tool's integration list. If it doesn't integrate, you'll have to manually enter dispatch data into your ELD, which is time-consuming.

## The Bottom Line

The best free dispatch tool depends on your volume. For owner-operators, TruckLogics is the top pick because it includes invoicing and IFTA, which are essential. For small fleets, Rose Rocket offers the most generous free plan with 10 loads and 3 drivers. If you need a load board, SuperTruckers or Truckstop TMS are good choices. But remember: free plans have limits. As your business grows, budget $30-$150 per month for a paid plan to get unlimited loads, GPS, and better support. Start with a free trial, test it with real loads, and upgrade when you hit the limits.
