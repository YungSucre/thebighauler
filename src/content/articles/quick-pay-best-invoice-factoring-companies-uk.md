If you run a trucking business in the UK, slow-paying customers can choke your cash flow. Invoice factoring lets you sell your unpaid invoices to a factor for an advance, usually 80-95% of the value, within 24 hours. This guide covers the best factoring companies for UK truckers, with realistic rates and steps to get started this week.

## How Invoice Factoring Works for Truckers

You deliver a load, invoice your customer, then submit that invoice to a factoring company. The factor advances you most of the invoice value, typically within 24 hours. When your customer pays the factor, you get the remaining balance minus a fee. This is not a loan; it's a sale of your receivables.

For trucking, factoring is especially useful because you often wait 30-60 days for payment from brokers or large shippers. Factoring bridges that gap, covering fuel, maintenance, and driver wages.

## Top UK Invoice Factoring Companies for Trucking

Here are the best options based on industry reputation, trucking-specific services, and transparent pricing. Rates vary with your invoice volume, customer creditworthiness, and contract length.

### 1. Bibby Financial Services

Bibby is a major player in UK factoring, with a dedicated transport team. They offer both recourse and non-recourse factoring. Their rates typically range from 1.5% to 3.5% of invoice value per month. They have no setup fees and offer same-day advances. Their online portal makes submitting invoices easy.

**Best for:** Small to medium fleets needing a reliable, established factor.

### 2. Skipton Business Finance

Skipton offers flexible factoring with no minimum turnover requirements. Their fees are competitive, often between 1.2% and 2.8% per month. They provide 24-hour advances and a dedicated account manager. They also offer confidential invoice discounting if you prefer not to tell your customers.

**Best for:** Owner-operators and small fleets looking for low minimums.

### 3. Lloyds Bank Invoice Finance

As part of a major bank, Lloyds offers factoring with added credibility. Their rates are typically 1.0% to 2.5% per month, but they require a minimum annual turnover of £100,000. They offer online account management and same-day funding. Their strength is integration with other banking services.

**Best for:** Established fleets with higher turnover who want a bank-backed option.

### 4. MarketInvoice (now part of HSBC)

MarketInvoice is an online platform that lets you sell individual invoices or a whole ledger. Pricing is transparent, with fees from 0.5% to 3% per invoice, depending on the customer's credit. You can choose which invoices to factor, giving you flexibility. They are ideal for tech-savvy truckers who want quick, on-demand funding.

**Best for:** Fleets that want to factor selectively, not all invoices.

### 5. Nucleus Commercial Finance

Nucleus offers factoring with a fast application process, often approving within 24 hours. Their rates range from 1.5% to 3% per month. They have no long-term contracts, which is a plus if you want flexibility. They also offer bad debt protection as an add-on.

**Best for:** Truckers who need quick setup and no lock-in.

### 6. A&T Business Finance

A&T specializes in transport and logistics, understanding the unique challenges of haulage. Their rates are competitive, from 1.2% to 2.5% per month. They offer 24-hour advances and a simple online portal. Their team has direct experience with trucking invoices, so they handle broker payments smoothly.

**Best for:** Truckers who want a factor that speaks their language.

## How to Choose the Right Factor for Your Trucking Business

1. **Check the contract length.** Some factors lock you in for 12 months. If you're unsure, choose a flexible option like Nucleus or MarketInvoice.
2. **Understand recourse vs. non-recourse.** Recourse means you buy back unpaid invoices; non-recourse protects you, but costs more. For most truckers, recourse is fine if your customers are reliable.
3. **Look at the advance rate.** Most offer 85-95% upfront. Higher advances mean less cash left at the end, but more immediate cash.
4. **Ask about hidden fees.** Some charge for setup, monthly minimums, or early termination. Get a full fee schedule.
5. **Check if they handle your customers well.** A factor that contacts your customers directly can strain relationships. Consider confidential invoice discounting if that's a concern.

## Steps to Get Started This Week

- **Gather your last 3 months of invoices** and a list of your top customers. Factors will assess their creditworthiness.
- **Get quotes from at least 3 companies.** Use the table above as a starting point. Ask for a sample contract.
- **Compare the effective cost.** Calculate the total fee on a typical £5,000 invoice. For example, at 2% per month, that's £100 for a 30-day term.
- **Check the advance speed.** Ask if they can fund same-day or next-day. In trucking, a day can make a difference.
- **Read the contract carefully.** Look for termination clauses and any minimum volume requirements.

## FAQ

### Is invoice factoring expensive for truckers?

It depends. Typical fees range from 1% to 3% per month. For a £5,000 invoice, that's £50 to £150. If you factor £50,000 per month, that's £500 to £1,500 in fees. Compare that to the cost of a late payment or a missed load due to cash shortage.

### Can I factor invoices from brokers?

Yes, but factors assess the credit of the broker. Some brokers have poor credit, which may increase your fee or be rejected. Work with factors that understand the transport industry, like A&T or Bibby.

### What happens if my customer doesn't pay?

With recourse factoring, you must buy back the invoice. With non-recourse, the factor absorbs the loss, but only if the customer goes bankrupt, not if they just dispute the invoice. Always read the terms.

### How fast can I get cash?

Most factors advance within 24 hours of submitting the invoice. Some offer same-day funding if you submit before a cutoff time, like Bibby and Lloyds.
