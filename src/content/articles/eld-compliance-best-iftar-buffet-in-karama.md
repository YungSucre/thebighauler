Looking for the best iftar buffet in Karama? You have plenty of solid options, from budget-friendly spots to upscale hotel spreads. This guide breaks down the top picks, what they cost, and what you can expect, so you can make a smart choice this Ramadan.

## Top Iftar Buffets in Karama

### 1. Al Ustad Special Kabab
A Karama institution, Al Ustad is famous for its Iranian kebabs, but during Ramadan they offer a generous iftar buffet that mixes traditional Persian and Indian dishes. Expect grilled meats, biryani, kebabs, and a range of curries, plus dates, soups, and sweets. The buffet is set up in the evenings, and the atmosphere is lively and family-friendly.

**Price range:** AED 45 to AED 60 per person (2026).

**What stands out:** The kebabs are cooked over open flames, and the buffet includes their signature mutton and chicken options. It is not a five-star spread, but the quality and value are hard to beat.

### 2. Calicut Paragon
If you want South Indian and Kerala-style dishes, Calicut Paragon delivers a robust iftar buffet. Their buffet features fish curry, chicken fry, appam, and a variety of vegetarian options, plus the usual dates, soup, and desserts. The restaurant is popular, so expect a crowd, especially on weekends.

**Price range:** AED 50 to AED 70 per person (2026).

**What stands out:** The seafood is fresh, and the flavors are authentic. They also offer a decent selection of North Indian dishes, so it works for mixed groups.

### 3. Bait Al Mandi
For Yemeni-style iftar, Bait Al Mandi is a strong choice. Their buffet includes mandi rice with chicken or lamb, salta, and a variety of Middle Eastern sides. The portions are generous, and the taste is consistent. It is a no-frills spot, but the food is hearty and satisfying.

**Price range:** AED 40 to AED 55 per person (2026).

**What stands out:** The mandi is cooked in a traditional tandoor, giving it a smoky flavor. They also offer fresh juices and traditional desserts like kunafa.

### 4. Karama Park Iftar Tents
If you want an outdoor experience, several iftar tents pop up around Karama Park during Ramadan. These are often run by local caterers and offer a simple buffet with rice, grilled chicken, curries, dates, and water. The atmosphere is communal, with long tables and a family vibe.

**Price range:** AED 25 to AED 40 per person (2026).

**What stands out:** It is the most budget-friendly option, and you get to break your fast in a park setting. The food is basic but filling, and it is a great way to meet people.

### 5. Golden Fork Restaurant
Golden Fork is a multi-cuisine spot that does a solid iftar buffet with Indian, Arabic, and Chinese dishes. The buffet includes live cooking stations for grills and pasta, plus a decent dessert section. It is a good middle-ground option if you want variety without the hotel price tag.

**Price range:** AED 60 to AED 80 per person (2026).

**What stands out:** The live stations add a bit of flair, and the dessert selection is better than most in the area. The seating is comfortable, and they handle large groups well.

### 6. Radisson Blu Hotel, Dubai Deira (Near Karama)
Just a short drive from Karama, the Radisson Blu offers a premium iftar buffet with a wide range of international cuisines, including Arabic, Indian, and continental. The spread includes seafood, grilled meats, and a lavish dessert table. It is a more upscale experience, with elegant decor and attentive service.

**Price range:** AED 120 to AED 150 per person (2026).

**What stands out:** The quality and variety are top-notch, and you get a more refined atmosphere. It is worth the extra cost if you are celebrating a special occasion.

## How to Choose the Right Buffet

When picking an iftar buffet in Karama, consider these factors:

- **Budget:** Set a per-person price before you go. Buffets range from AED 25 to AED 150, so know what you are willing to spend.
- **Cuisine preference:** Do you want Indian, Arabic, or a mix? Karama has strong South Indian and Middle Eastern options.
- **Group size:** Some places are better for large groups, like Calicut Paragon or Golden Fork, while others are more intimate.
- **Atmosphere:** If you want a quiet meal, avoid the busiest times (around sunset). If you enjoy buzz, go early.
- **Reservations:** Many popular spots fill up fast, especially on weekends. Call ahead or book online through apps like Zomato or Talabat.

## Practical Tips for a Smooth Iftar Experience

- **Arrive 15 minutes early:** This gives you time to settle in and grab a spot before the rush.
- **Break your fast with dates and water first:** This is the Sunnah and helps your stomach adjust.
- **Pace yourself:** Buffets can lead to overeating. Start with soup and salad, then move to mains.
- **Check if the buffet includes drinks:** Some places charge extra for juices or soft drinks. Ask before you order.
- **Use delivery apps for takeaway if you prefer to eat at home:** Many restaurants offer iftar boxes for delivery, which can be cheaper and more convenient.

## FAQ

### Are iftar buffets in Karama open to non-Muslims?
Yes, all these restaurants are open to everyone. You do not need to be Muslim to enjoy the buffet. Just respect the fasting hours and the atmosphere.

### Do I need to book a table in advance?
It is highly recommended, especially for popular spots like Al Ustad or Calicut Paragon. You can book by phone or through apps like Zomato. Walk-ins are possible, but you may wait.

### What is the average cost of an iftar buffet in Karama?
In 2026, prices range from AED 25 at park tents to AED 150 at hotel buffets. Most mid-range restaurants charge between AED 45 and AED 80 per person.

### Can I get a takeaway iftar instead of dining in?
Yes, many restaurants offer iftar boxes or takeaway options. Check with the restaurant directly, or use delivery apps like Talabat or Deliveroo.

## The Bottom Line

The best iftar buffet in Karama depends on your budget and taste. For authentic kebabs, go to Al Ustad Special Kabab. For South Indian flavors, Calicut Paragon is a winner. If you want a budget-friendly outdoor experience, the Karama Park tents are a great choice. And if you are willing to spend more, the Radisson Blu offers a premium spread. Book ahead, arrive early, and enjoy the meal. Ramadan is about community and gratitude, so focus on the experience, not just the food.
