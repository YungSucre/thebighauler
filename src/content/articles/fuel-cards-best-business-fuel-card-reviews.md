If you're running a truck, fuel is your biggest variable cost. A good business fuel card can save you $0.10 to $0.50 per gallon, but the wrong one can cost you in fees and limited acceptance. This guide reviews the top fuel cards for owner-operators and small fleets, with realistic 2026 pricing and features. We'll help you match a card to your driving patterns and cash flow needs.

## How We Picked These Cards

We focused on cards that offer real discounts, wide acceptance, and transparent fees. We looked at transaction costs, billing cycles, and extra features like fuel spend controls and discounts on maintenance. We also considered how easy it is to integrate with dispatch and accounting software. All figures are as of early 2026 and can vary by region and volume.

## The Top Business Fuel Cards

### 1. Fleet One OTR Edge

**Best for:** Owner-operators who want cash back and no monthly fee.

Fleet One OTR Edge offers up to $0.10 per gallon discount at in-network truck stops, plus 2% cash back on non-fuel purchases. There is no monthly fee, but you'll pay a $4.95 transaction fee per fuel purchase. The card works at major truck stops like Pilot Flying J, Love's, and TA/Petro. You get free online account management and fuel price comparison tools.

**Pros:** No monthly fee, cash back on non-fuel, wide acceptance.
**Cons:** Transaction fee adds up if you fuel daily.

### 2. EFS Fuel Card

**Best for:** Small fleets with multiple drivers and need for controls.

EFS (Electronic Funds Source) offers a customizable card with per-driver spending limits, fuel type restrictions, and real-time alerts. Discounts range from $0.05 to $0.15 per gallon depending on volume. There's a $5 monthly fee per card, and transaction fees start at $3.50. EFS integrates with popular TMS software like TruckLogics and Rigbooks.

**Pros:** Strong controls, TMS integration, good for fleets.
**Cons:** Monthly fee per card, transaction fees.

### 3. Comdata Fuel Card

**Best for:** High-volume fleets and over-the-road drivers.

Comdata is a industry standard with over 5,000 truck stop locations. Discounts are negotiated based on volume, often $0.10 to $0.25 per gallon. There's a $7 monthly fee per card and a $4.50 transaction fee. Comdata offers fuel price management, driver spend controls, and a mobile app. It also provides fuel tax reporting, which is a plus for IFTA compliance.

**Pros:** Huge network, robust reporting, fuel tax help.
**Cons:** Higher fees, discount requires volume.

### 4. WEX Fuel Card

**Best for:** Fleets that want flexible billing and fuel discounts.

WEX offers two main cards: the WEX Fuel Card and the WEX OTR Fuel Card. The OTR card is designed for long-haul trucking with discounts up to $0.15 per gallon at participating locations. Monthly fee is $6 per card, transaction fee $3.95. WEX provides detailed fuel reports and integration with accounting software. It also offers a fuel price index to help you find cheap diesel.

**Pros:** Good integration, fuel price index, flexible billing.
**Cons:** Fees can be high for small operations.

### 5. TCS Fuel Card

**Best for:** Owner-operators who want no transaction fees.

TCS (Transport Clearing Services) offers a fuel card with no transaction fees and discounts up to $0.10 per gallon. There's a $10 monthly fee, but you can waive it by using the card for at least 10 fuel purchases a month. TCS has a network of over 2,000 truck stops, including many independent stations. It also offers a cash price option at some locations.

**Pros:** No transaction fees, simple pricing, good for independents.
**Cons:** Smaller network, monthly fee unless you meet usage.

### 6. Fuelman by FleetCor

**Best for:** Small fleets that want easy fuel management.

Fuelman offers a straightforward fuel card with discounts at over 7,000 locations, including many regional chains. You can set per-driver limits and get real-time alerts. Monthly fee is $5 per card, transaction fee $3.50. Fuelman provides a mobile app and online dashboard. Discounts are typically $0.05 to $0.10 per gallon.

**Pros:** Easy to use, good for small fleets, no long-term contract.
**Cons:** Discounts are lower, network not as large as Comdata.

## How to Choose the Right Card

Start by calculating your monthly fuel spend. If you buy 2,000 gallons a month, a $0.10 discount saves $200. Compare that to fees: if you fuel 20 times a month, a $4.95 transaction fee costs $99. So a card with no monthly fee but high transaction fees might still be better than one with a low transaction fee but high monthly fee.

Next, check the network. If you run regional routes, make sure the card covers the truck stops you use. If you're OTR, a larger network like Comdata or WEX is safer.

Finally, consider extra features. Do you need fuel tax reporting? Do you want to set spending limits for drivers? Do you need integration with your accounting software? These features can save you hours of admin time.

## FAQ

### Can I use a business fuel card for personal vehicles?

Most fuel cards are for commercial vehicles only. Using them for personal vehicles may violate the terms and lead to account closure. If you need a card for both, look for one that allows mixed use, but expect higher fees.

### Do fuel cards affect my credit score?

Most fuel cards are charge cards, not credit cards, so they don't report to credit bureaus. However, some providers may do a hard pull when you apply, which can temporarily lower your score. Ask before applying.

### How do fuel discounts compare to cash prices?

Fuel card discounts are usually off the credit price, not the cash price. At many truck stops, the cash price is already $0.10 to $0.20 cheaper than credit. So your effective savings might be less than advertised. Always compare the card price to the cash price at your regular stops.

### Can I negotiate a better discount?

Yes, especially if you have a fleet or high volume. Call the provider and ask for a better rate. Mention competing offers. Many will match or beat them to win your business.

## The Bottom Line

The best business fuel card depends on your driving pattern and volume. For owner-operators, Fleet One OTR Edge or TCS can save money with no transaction fees. For small fleets, EFS or Fuelman offer good controls at a reasonable cost. For high-volume operations, Comdata or WEX provide the best discounts and network coverage. Do the math with your actual numbers, and don't be afraid to negotiate. A few hours of research can save you thousands a year.
