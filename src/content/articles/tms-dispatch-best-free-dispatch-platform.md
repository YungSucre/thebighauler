If you're a small fleet owner or owner-operator looking to streamline dispatch without spending a dime, free dispatch platforms can handle the basics: load assignment, driver communication, and basic tracking. But free tiers come with limits, and the best choice depends on your fleet size and needs. Here are the top free options in 2026, what they offer, and how to pick one this week.

## 1. TruckLogics (Free Plan)

TruckLogics offers a free plan for owner-operators and small fleets (up to 2 trucks). It includes dispatch, basic accounting, IFTA fuel tax reporting, and driver settlement. The free tier is limited to 2 trucks and 1 user, but it's a solid all-in-one for a solo driver or a driver with one truck.

- **Free features:** Dispatch board, load tracking, fuel log, IFTA calculation, basic invoicing.
- **Limits:** 2 trucks max, 1 user, limited reporting.
- **Paid upgrade:** $35-$50/month per truck for more trucks and features.

## 2. Axon Software (Free Version)

Axon has a free version for up to 2 trucks and 2 users. It's a full TMS with dispatch, accounting, and driver settlement. The free version is surprisingly robust, but it lacks some advanced features like automatic fuel tax filing and mobile app access.

- **Free features:** Dispatch, load tracking, invoicing, settlement, basic reports.
- **Limits:** 2 trucks, 2 users, no mobile app, no fuel tax filing.
- **Paid upgrade:** $50-$70/month per truck for full features.

## 3. TruckingOffice (Free Trial vs. Free Plan)

TruckingOffice does not have a permanent free plan, but it offers a 30-day free trial with full features. For a one-time or short-term need, the trial is useful. After the trial, plans start at $35/month for 1 truck. If you need a free option long-term, this isn't it, but it's worth testing.

- **Free trial:** 30 days, full features.
- **Limits:** Time-limited.
- **Paid plans:** $35-$60/month per truck.

## 4. Google Sheets + Free Apps (DIY Approach)

Many owner-operators build a simple dispatch board using Google Sheets, combined with free communication apps like WhatsApp or Telegram. This is truly free and flexible, but it lacks automation and integration. You'll manually update load status, track hours, and handle billing.

- **Free features:** Unlimited trucks (as long as you manage it), custom fields, real-time collaboration.
- **Limits:** No automation, no IFTA calculation, no integrated accounting, requires manual data entry.
- **Cost:** $0, but your time is the cost.

## 5. Dispatch.io (Free Tier for Small Fleets)

Dispatch.io offers a free tier for up to 3 trucks and 2 users. It focuses on dispatch and driver communication, with a mobile app for drivers. The free tier includes load assignment, status updates, and basic reporting.

- **Free features:** Dispatch board, driver app, load tracking, basic reports.
- **Limits:** 3 trucks, 2 users, no accounting or IFTA.
- **Paid upgrade:** $40-$60/month per truck for full TMS features.

## How to Choose the Right Free Dispatch Platform

1. **Assess your fleet size:** If you have 1-2 trucks, TruckLogics or Axon are solid. If you have 3 trucks, Dispatch.io's free tier fits.
2. **Identify must-have features:** Do you need IFTA calculation? If yes, TruckLogics. Do you need a mobile app for drivers? Dispatch.io. Do you need accounting? Axon.
3. **Test with a real load:** This week, create a test load in the platform you're considering. Enter a load, assign a driver, and track it. See if the workflow feels natural.
4. **Check integration with your other tools:** If you use QuickBooks, see if the platform integrates. Many free tiers don't.
5. **Plan for growth:** If you expect to add trucks soon, choose a platform that scales affordably. Free tiers are limited, so you'll likely upgrade.

## FAQ

**Q: Are free dispatch platforms really free?**
A: Yes, they are free to use within the limits (e.g., number of trucks, users). No credit card required for most. But they may show ads or have limited support.

**Q: Can I use a free platform for IFTA reporting?**
A: Some do, like TruckLogics and Axon. But free versions may not include automatic filing, only calculation. You'll still need to submit to your state.

**Q: What's the catch with free tiers?**
A: Limits on trucks, users, and features. You may outgrow them quickly. Also, support is often limited or nonexistent.

**Q: Can I switch from a free platform to a paid one later?**
A: Yes, most platforms allow you to upgrade without losing data. Check the upgrade process before committing.

## The Bottom Line

The best free dispatch platform depends on your fleet size and needs. For a solo operator, TruckLogics is a strong all-rounder. For a small fleet of up to 3 trucks, Dispatch.io is worth a look. If you're comfortable with manual work, Google Sheets is truly free and unlimited. Start with a free trial or free tier, test it with a real load, and upgrade when you need more. The goal is to save time and reduce errors, so pick the one that fits your workflow best.
