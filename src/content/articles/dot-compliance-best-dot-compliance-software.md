If you're a truck driver, owner-operator, or small fleet owner, DOT compliance software isn't optional. It's how you stay legal, avoid fines, and keep your operation running smoothly. The best software handles ELD logs, IFTA fuel taxes, vehicle maintenance, and driver qualification files in one place. But with so many options, how do you choose? This guide breaks down the top DOT compliance software for 2026, with real pricing and practical steps to get started this week.

## What to Look for in DOT Compliance Software

Before diving into specific products, know what matters most. The right software should cover these core areas:

- **ELD compliance**: Automatic logging, unassigned driving time, and roadside inspection mode.
- **IFTA reporting**: Fuel tax calculations and state-by-state mileage tracking.
- **DVIR (Driver Vehicle Inspection Reports)**: Digital pre-trip and post-trip inspections.
- **Maintenance tracking**: Alerts for oil changes, tire rotations, and DOT inspections.
- **Driver qualification files**: Track licenses, medical cards, and endorsements.
- **User-friendliness**: A mobile app that drivers actually use without constant training.
- **Support**: 24/7 customer service, because breakdowns don't wait for business hours.

Price ranges vary widely. Expect to pay $30-$50 per vehicle per month for basic ELD plans, and $50-$100 per vehicle per month for full compliance suites. Some providers offer discounts for annual billing or multi-year contracts.

## Top DOT Compliance Software Options

Here are six leading platforms, compared by features and pricing. Prices are as of early 2026 and may vary based on fleet size and add-ons.

### 1. Samsara

Samsara is a top choice for fleets that want an all-in-one platform. It offers ELD, IFTA, maintenance, and even dashcams. The mobile app is intuitive, and the dashboard gives real-time visibility into your entire fleet.

- **Best for**: Fleets of 5 or more vehicles that want advanced telematics.
- **Pricing**: Starts at $35 per vehicle per month for ELD only; full compliance suite runs $55-$75 per vehicle per month.
- **Key features**: AI dashcams, GPS tracking, automated IFTA reports, and maintenance alerts.

### 2. Motive (formerly KeepTruckin)

Motive is popular among owner-operators and small fleets for its affordable pricing and easy setup. It covers ELD, IFTA, and DVIRs, and offers a simple driver app.

- **Best for**: Owner-operators and fleets under 20 trucks.
- **Pricing**: ELD starts at $20 per vehicle per month; the full compliance plan is around $45 per vehicle per month.
- **Key features**: FMCSA-compliant ELD, automatic IFTA mileage, and 24/7 support.

### 3. Geotab

Geotab is a robust platform that integrates with many third-party apps. It's more complex but highly customizable. You'll need a hardware device installed in each vehicle.

- **Best for**: Larger fleets that need deep data integration.
- **Pricing**: Hardware costs $150-$300 per vehicle, plus a subscription of $20-$40 per vehicle per month. Full compliance features may add $20-$30 more.
- **Key features**: Advanced analytics, customizable reports, and integration with maintenance systems.

### 4. Fleet Complete

Fleet Complete offers a solid balance of features and price. It's known for strong IFTA and fuel tax reporting, which is a pain point for many drivers.

- **Best for**: Fleets that run across multiple states and need precise fuel tax tracking.
- **Pricing**: ELD starts at $25 per vehicle per month; complete compliance package is $50-$65 per vehicle per month.
- **Key features**: IFTA automation, GPS tracking, and maintenance scheduling.

### 5. Verizon Connect

Verizon Connect is a big name with a comprehensive suite. It's reliable but can be pricey, especially for small fleets. You get professional-grade support and a polished interface.

- **Best for**: Fleets that prioritize support and brand reliability.
- **Pricing**: ELD plans start at $30 per vehicle per month; full compliance can run $60-$90 per vehicle per month.
- **Key features**: Real-time tracking, DVIR, and driver behavior monitoring.

### 6. EROAD

EROAD is a solid budget option, especially for owner-operators. It has a simple ELD device and a straightforward app. IFTA reporting is included in most plans.

- **Best for**: Single-truck operations that need low-cost compliance.
- **Pricing**: ELD starts at $15 per vehicle per month; full compliance is around $35 per vehicle per month.
- **Key features**: Simple ELD, automatic IFTA, and no long-term contracts.

## How to Choose the Right Software for You

Follow these steps to make the right decision without wasting money:

1. **List your must-haves**: Do you need IFTA? Dashcams? Maintenance alerts? Write down your top 3 priorities.
2. **Set a budget**: Decide what you can pay per vehicle per month. Remember, the cheapest option isn't always the best if it lacks features you need.
3. **Read reviews from drivers**: Check forums like TruckersReport or Reddit's r/Truckers for real-world experiences. Look for complaints about app crashes or poor customer service.
4. **Take advantage of free trials**: Most providers offer 30-day free trials. Test the app on your phone and see how easy it is to log hours, submit DVIRs, and generate IFTA reports.
5. **Check hardware requirements**: Some systems require professional installation. If you're an owner-operator, you might prefer a plug-and-play device like Motive's or EROAD's.
6. **Ask about contract terms**: Some companies lock you in for a year. Others offer month-to-month. Choose what fits your business model.

## FAQ

### Do I need DOT compliance software if I'm an owner-operator?

Yes, if you operate a commercial vehicle requiring an ELD, you must use an FMCSA-approved ELD. Software that also handles IFTA and maintenance can save you hours each month and prevent costly fines.

### Can I use the same software for ELD and IFTA?

Yes, most full compliance suites include IFTA reporting. They automatically track miles and fuel purchases, which makes quarterly tax filing much easier.

### How much does DOT compliance software cost per month?

For a single truck, expect to pay $15-$50 per month for ELD-only plans. Full compliance packages range from $35-$90 per vehicle per month, depending on features and fleet size.

### What happens if my software doesn't meet FMCSA regulations?

You could face fines during roadside inspections or audits. Always verify that the software is FMCSA-certified. Check the FMCSA's registered ELD list before purchasing.

## The Bottom Line

Start with a free trial, test the app, and make sure it fits your workflow. The right software will save you time, reduce stress, and keep you compliant on every mile.
