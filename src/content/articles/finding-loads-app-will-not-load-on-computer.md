When a load board, dispatch, or ELD app won't load on your computer, it can stop your day cold. The fix is usually simple: a bad internet connection, an outdated browser, or a corrupted cache. This guide walks you through the most common causes and the exact steps to get back online, with realistic timeframes and costs.

## Check Your Internet Connection First

A weak or dropped connection is the #1 reason apps fail to load. Do this before touching any settings:

- Open a second tab and go to a simple site like example.com. If it loads slowly or not at all, your connection is the problem.
- Restart your router and modem: unplug both, wait 60 seconds, plug the modem back in, then the router. Wait 2 minutes for a full reboot.
- If you're on cellular hotspot or a truck stop Wi-Fi, move to a spot with better signal. A signal booster costs $200-$400 and can save hours.
- Test your speed: a load board needs at least 5 Mbps download and 2 Mbps upload. Use a free site like Speedtest.net. If you're below that, your connection is too weak.

If the internet is fine, move to the next step.

## Clear Your Browser Cache and Cookies

Outdated cache files often cause apps to hang or show a blank screen. This is a common fix and takes under 5 minutes.

- In Chrome: press Ctrl+Shift+Delete (Windows) or Cmd+Shift+Delete (Mac). Select "Cached images and files" and "Cookies and other site data." Choose "All time," then click "Clear data."
- In Firefox: press Ctrl+Shift+Delete, choose "Cache" and "Cookies," then click "Clear."
- In Edge: same shortcut, select "Cached images and files" and "Cookies," then "Clear now."

After clearing, close the browser completely and reopen it. Log back into the app. If the app still won't load, try a different browser.

## Try a Different Browser or Update Your Current One

Some apps are built for specific browsers. If you're using an old version, the app may not render properly.

- Update your browser: go to the menu (three dots or lines), find "About" or "Help," and check for updates. Chrome, Firefox, and Edge all auto-update, but a manual check is quick.
- Try a different browser: if you're on Chrome, switch to Firefox or Edge. Most load boards work on all major browsers, but some have quirks.
- Disable browser extensions: ad blockers or privacy extensions can block app scripts. In Chrome, go to Settings > Extensions, and toggle off all extensions. Reload the app. If it works, turn extensions back on one by one to find the culprit.

If the app still fails, move to the next step.

## Restart Your Computer and Update Your Operating System

A simple restart clears memory and stops background processes that might interfere. Do this if you haven't in a few days.

- Save any open work, then click Start > Restart (Windows) or Apple menu > Restart (Mac).
- After restart, check for OS updates: Windows Update or System Settings > Software Update on Mac. Install any pending updates, then restart again.
- Outdated OS versions can cause compatibility issues with newer app versions. Most apps require Windows 10 or 11, or macOS 11 or later.

If the app still won't load, the problem may be on the app's side.

## Check the App's Server Status

Sometimes the app itself is down. This happens with load boards and dispatch systems, especially during peak hours.

- Visit the app's official website or social media page (Twitter/X, Facebook) for outage announcements.
- Use a site like Downdetector.com to see if other users report issues.
- If the server is down, you can't fix it. Wait 30-60 minutes and try again. You can also call the app's support line to confirm.

## Reinstall the App or Use the Web Version

If the app is a desktop client (like a downloaded program), a corrupted installation can cause loading failures. Reinstalling is a solid fix.

- Uninstall the app: go to Control Panel > Programs > Uninstall a Program (Windows) or drag the app to Trash (Mac).
- Download the latest version from the official website. Avoid third-party download sites.
- Install and log back in. This takes 10-20 minutes.

Many trucking apps also have a web version that runs in your browser. If the desktop app fails, try the web version as a temporary workaround. For example, if you use a load board like DAT or Truckstop, their web portals often work when the desktop app doesn't.

## Check Your Firewall and Antivirus Settings

Security software can block an app from connecting to the internet, even if it looks like it's loading.

- Temporarily disable your antivirus and firewall (Windows Defender, Norton, McAfee, etc.) and try to load the app.
- If it works, add the app to the exceptions list. In Windows Defender, go to Settings > Privacy & Security > Windows Security > Firewall & network protection > Allow an app through firewall.
- For third-party antivirus, look for "exceptions" or "allow list" settings. Add the app's executable file (usually in Program Files or Applications).
- Re-enable your security software after testing.

## When to Call Support

If you've tried all the steps above and the app still won't load, it's time to get help. Most load boards and dispatch apps have 24/7 support for drivers.

- Look for a "Contact" or "Support" link in the app or on the website.
- Call the support number and explain what you've done. Have your account info ready.
- Ask if there's a known issue or if they can reset your session on their end.

Support response times vary: some answer in minutes, others in hours. If it's urgent, mention that you're a driver trying to book a load.

## FAQ

### Why does my load board app only fail on my laptop but work on my phone?

This usually points to a browser or OS issue on your laptop. Try clearing cache, updating the browser, or using a different browser. If the app is a desktop client, reinstall it. Your phone likely uses a different browser or app version, so it may not have the same problem.

### How do I know if the app is down vs. my computer?

Check Downdetector.com or the app's social media. If other users report issues, it's the app. Also, try loading the app on your phone using the same Wi-Fi. If it works on your phone, the problem is your computer.

### Can a VPN cause an app to not load?

Yes. VPNs can route traffic through servers that the app blocks. Disconnect your VPN and try again. If the app loads, either keep the VPN off or switch to a different server location.

### What's the fastest fix if I need to book a load right now?

Use the app's web version on your browser, or call the load board's support line to book over the phone. Many carriers also have a mobile app that might work even if the desktop app fails.

## The bottom line

A load board or dispatch app that won't load is frustrating, but most fixes are quick and free. Start with your internet connection, then clear your cache, update your browser, and restart your computer. If that fails, check the app's server status and reinstall if needed. In a pinch, use the web version or call support. If you're a small fleet owner, consider having a backup plan, like a mobile hotspot or a second browser, so a glitch doesn't cost you a load. With these steps, you'll be back to booking loads in under 30 minutes in most cases.
