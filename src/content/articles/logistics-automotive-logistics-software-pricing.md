Automotive logistics software pricing varies widely, but you can expect to pay $50 to $500 per user per month for a TMS, plus setup and integration fees. For a small fleet or brokerage, a solid system typically runs $500 to $2,500 per month total, while enterprise solutions can exceed $10,000 per month. This guide breaks down the costs by software type, deployment model, and features, so you can budget accurately and avoid sticker shock.

## What Drives the Price?

Several factors determine what you'll pay:

- **Deployment model:** Cloud (SaaS) is usually a monthly subscription; on-premise requires a large upfront license fee (often $50,000+).
- **Number of users:** Most vendors charge per user, but some have flat rates for unlimited users.
- **Modules and features:** Core TMS, WMS, ELD, driver app, customer portal, and analytics all add cost.
- **Integrations:** Connecting to your accounting, ERP, or load boards may cost extra.
- **Support and training:** Premium support and onboarding can add 10-20% to the annual cost.
- **Volume:** Higher shipment volumes often unlock lower per-load pricing.

## TMS Pricing Models

Transportation Management Systems (TMS) are the core of logistics software. Here are the common pricing models:

- **Per user per month:** $50 to $150 per user for basic TMS; $150 to $300 for advanced features like route optimization and real-time tracking.
- **Per load:** $1 to $5 per load, often with a minimum monthly fee. Good for low-volume shippers.
- **Flat monthly fee:** $500 to $5,000 per month, depending on features and number of trucks.
- **Tiered plans:** Basic, Professional, Enterprise. Basic might be $99/user/month, Professional $199, Enterprise custom.

## WMS Pricing

Warehouse Management Systems (WMS) are priced differently, often based on warehouse size or number of orders:

- **Per user per month:** $50 to $200 per user.
- **Per order:** $0.10 to $0.50 per order, with a minimum monthly fee.
- **Flat monthly fee:** $1,000 to $10,000 per month for mid-sized warehouses.
- **One-time license:** $50,000 to $500,000 for on-premise WMS.

## Fleet Management and ELD Pricing

Fleet management software often includes ELD compliance, GPS tracking, and maintenance logs. Pricing:

- **Basic ELD:** $20 to $40 per vehicle per month.
- **Fleet management with ELD:** $30 to $80 per vehicle per month.
- **Advanced telematics (video, sensors):** $50 to $150 per vehicle per month.

## Hidden Costs to Watch For

- **Setup and implementation:** $500 to $5,000, sometimes waived for annual contracts.
- **Integration fees:** $100 to $500 per integration.
- **Training:** $200 to $1,000 per session.
- **Data migration:** $500 to $2,000.
- **Hardware (if required):** Tablets, ELD devices, sensors can cost $200 to $500 per vehicle.
- **Overages:** If you exceed user or load limits, expect extra charges.

## How to Get Accurate Quotes

1. **List your must-have features** (e.g., ELD integration, load tracking, invoicing).
2. **Define your volume:** number of loads per month, trucks, users.
3. **Ask for a detailed quote** that includes setup, training, and support.
4. **Request a free trial** (most vendors offer 14-30 days).
5. **Negotiate:** Annual contracts often get 10-20% off.

## ROI: When Does Software Pay for Itself?

A good TMS can save you money in several ways:

- **Reduced admin time:** Save 5-10 hours per week per dispatcher, worth $500-$1,000/month.
- **Better load utilization:** Increase revenue per load by 5-10%.
- **Fewer errors:** Reduce billing errors and late fees.
- **Improved customer service:** Faster responses and tracking lead to repeat business.

If your software costs $1,000/month and saves 10 hours of admin time (worth $250/week), you break even in the first month.

## FAQ

**Q: Is there free automotive logistics software?**
A: Yes, some TMS platforms offer free tiers for small fleets (e.g., AscendTMS free version). But they often lack advanced features like automated dispatch or customer portals. For a growing operation, expect to pay.

**Q: How much does a TMS cost for a small fleet of 5 trucks?**
A: For 5 trucks, you might pay $300 to $750 per month for a basic TMS with ELD integration, plus $100-$200 per truck for ELD hardware if not included.

**Q: Are there per-load pricing options?**
A: Yes, some TMS providers charge $1 to $5 per load, which is ideal for low-volume shippers. For example, if you move 100 loads a month, you might pay $200-$500.

**Q: What's the difference between TMS and WMS pricing?**
A: TMS is typically per user or per load, while WMS is often per order or per warehouse square footage. TMS focuses on transportation, WMS on warehouse operations.

## The Bottom Line

Automotive logistics software is a significant investment, but the right system pays for itself through efficiency gains. Start by defining your needs, get quotes from at least three vendors, and negotiate. Remember to factor in setup, training, and hardware costs. For most small to mid-sized operations, a budget of $500 to $2,500 per month is realistic for a robust TMS. Don't overpay for features you won't use, but don't skimp on core functions like ELD compliance and load tracking. Take advantage of free trials to test before you commit.
