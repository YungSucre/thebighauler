Finding the best iftar buffet in Islamabad means balancing variety, quality, and value. This guide covers the top options for 2026, with realistic price ranges and what to expect at each. Whether you're a local or just passing through during Ramadan, these picks offer a reliable way to break your fast without guesswork.

## 1. Serena Hotel: The Classic Choice

Serena Hotel's iftar buffet is a staple for those who want a premium experience. Expect a lavish spread with live cooking stations, traditional Pakistani dishes, and international options. The ambiance is upscale, and service is attentive.

- **Price:** PKR 4,500 to PKR 6,000 per person (2026)
- **Highlights:** Live BBQ, chaat counter, and a wide dessert selection including jalebi and kheer.
- **Tip:** Book at least 3 days in advance, especially on weekends.

## 2. Monal Restaurant: Great Views, Solid Buffet

Perched on the Margalla Hills, Monal offers a stunning view of Islamabad along with a decent iftar buffet. The menu focuses on Pakistani and Afghan cuisine, with generous portions.

- **Price:** PKR 2,800 to PKR 3,500 per person
- **Highlights:** Grilled kebabs, lamb karahi, and fresh naan from the tandoor.
- **Tip:** Arrive 30 minutes before iftar to secure a table with a view; the drive up can be slow during Ramadan.

## 3. The Marquee: Family-Friendly and Extensive

The Marquee is known for its massive buffet spread, making it a favorite for families. It offers a mix of desi and continental dishes, plus a dedicated iftar items section with dates, pakoras, and samosas.

- **Price:** PKR 3,200 to PKR 4,000 per person
- **Highlights:** Live pasta station, Chinese corner, and a wide variety of sweets.
- **Tip:** They offer a family package on weekdays; check their social media for current deals.

## 4. Ramada Hotel: Value for Money

Ramada's iftar buffet is a budget-friendly option without compromising on quality. It's less crowded than Serena, making it a good choice for a relaxed meal.

- **Price:** PKR 2,500 to PKR 3,200 per person
- **Highlights:** Good selection of BBQ, traditional Pakistani curries, and a decent dessert spread.
- **Tip:** Look for online coupons or credit card offers to save up to 20%.

## 5. Street Food at F-7 and F-10 Markets: The Budget Route

If you prefer a more casual and affordable iftar, the F-7 and F-10 markets come alive with street vendors selling chaat, pakoras, samosas, and fruit chaat. You can eat well for under PKR 1,000 per person.

- **Price:** PKR 500 to PKR 1,000 per person
- **Highlights:** Authentic local flavors, quick service, and a vibrant atmosphere.
- **Tip:** Go early (around 6 PM) to avoid the rush and get the freshest items.

## FAQ

### 1. Do I need to book in advance for iftar buffets?

Yes, especially for popular spots like Serena and Monal. Book at least 2 to 3 days ahead, particularly for weekends or the last 10 days of Ramadan.

### 2. Are there separate family sections?

Most hotels and restaurants offer family sections. The Marquee and Serena have dedicated family areas. It's best to request one when booking.

### 3. What time does iftar buffet start?

Buffets typically start 30 minutes before Maghrib prayer and run until about 9 PM. Arrive early to enjoy the full spread.

### 4. Are there options for vegetarians?

Yes, most buffets include vegetarian dishes like chana masala, vegetable biryani, and salads. Street vendors also offer veggie pakoras and chaat.

## The Bottom Line

The best iftar buffet in Islamabad depends on your budget and preference. For a premium experience, Serena Hotel is unmatched. For value, Ramada offers a solid meal. If you want views, head to Monal. And if you're on a tight budget, the street food at F-7 and F-10 gives you an authentic taste without breaking the bank. Whichever you choose, book ahead and arrive early to make the most of your iftar.
