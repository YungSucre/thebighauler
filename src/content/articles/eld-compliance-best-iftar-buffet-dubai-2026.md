Looking for the best iftar buffet in Dubai in 2026? You have plenty of choices, from luxury hotel spreads to more affordable local favorites. This guide covers the top options with realistic price ranges and practical booking advice, so you can enjoy a memorable iftar without the guesswork.

## Top Iftar Buffets in Dubai 2026

Here are the standout iftar buffets for 2026, selected for food quality, ambiance, and value. Prices are per person and include water and basic beverages unless noted. Expect to pay AED 150 to AED 400 for most buffet iftars, with luxury options going higher.

### 1. Al Hadheerah at Bab Al Shams Desert Resort

This is a full desert experience, not just a meal. Set in a desert village, it features live cooking stations, Arabic grills, and traditional entertainment. The buffet includes over 50 dishes, from mezze to whole roasted lamb. Price: AED 395 per adult, AED 175 for children 6-12. Booking is essential, especially on weekends.

### 2. Asado at The Palace Downtown

Overlooking the Burj Khalifa and Dubai Fountain, Asado offers a South American twist on iftar. The buffet includes grilled meats, seafood, and a wide selection of Arabic mezze. Price: AED 320 per person. The fountain show adds to the experience, making it a great choice for families or couples.

### 3. Saffron at Atlantis The Palm

Saffron is known for its massive buffet with 20+ live stations covering Asian, Middle Eastern, and international cuisines. The iftar spread includes Arabic specialities, sushi, and a dedicated dessert corner. Price: AED 295 per person. It is popular, so book at least a week ahead.

### 4. The Market Place at Jumeirah Beach Hotel

This buffet offers a relaxed, family-friendly atmosphere with views of the beach and Burj Al Arab. The iftar includes a mix of Arabic and international dishes, with a live cooking station for grills and pasta. Price: AED 260 per adult, AED 130 for children. Good value for the location.

### 5. Al Dawaar at Hyatt Regency Dubai

Located on the 25th floor, this revolving restaurant gives you panoramic city views. The iftar buffet focuses on Middle Eastern cuisine, with a good selection of hot and cold mezze, grills, and desserts. Price: AED 240 per person. The revolving floor is a unique touch.

### 6. Local Favorites: Al Ustad Special Kabab and Ravi Restaurant

If you want a more budget-friendly iftar, these local spots offer authentic Pakistani and Iranian dishes. Al Ustad Special Kabab (near Al Fahidi) serves juicy kebabs and fresh bread, with a full meal for around AED 40-60 per person. Ravi Restaurant (Deira) is a legendary spot for Pakistani curries and grilled meats, with iftar specials around AED 30-50. These are not buffets, but they are excellent for a casual iftar.

## How to Choose the Right Iftar Buffet

Consider these factors when picking your iftar:

- **Budget**: Set a per-person price. Buffets range from AED 150 to AED 400. Local restaurants are cheaper but not buffet-style.
- **Cuisine**: Do you want Arabic, international, or a specific theme? Check the menu online.
- **Location**: Choose a spot close to your home or work to avoid traffic during rush hour.
- **Ambiance**: Luxury hotels offer grand settings, while local spots are more casual.
- **Family-friendliness**: Some buffets have kids' pricing and activities. Check before booking.

## Booking Tips for Ramadan 2026

Ramadan 2026 is expected to start around February 18 and end around March 19, but exact dates depend on the moon sighting. Iftar time will be around 6:30 PM at the start, moving to about 7:00 PM by the end.

- **Book early**: Popular buffets fill up fast, especially on weekends. Reserve at least 2-3 weeks ahead.
- **Use online platforms**: Check websites like Platinumlist or the hotel's own booking page for offers and discounts.
- **Ask about cancellation policies**: Some hotels require prepayment or charge a fee for no-shows.
- **Arrive early**: Aim to be at the restaurant 30 minutes before iftar to get a good table and avoid the rush.
- **Check for special deals**: Many hotels offer early-bird discounts or family packages. Look for promo codes.

## FAQ

### Do I need to book in advance for iftar buffets in Dubai?

Yes, especially for popular hotel buffets. Book at least a week ahead, and 2-3 weeks for weekends or the last ten days of Ramadan.

### Are iftar buffets in Dubai expensive?

Hotel buffets range from AED 240 to AED 400 per person. Local restaurants offer meals for under AED 60, but they are not buffets.

### Can I find vegetarian options at these buffets?

Yes, most buffets have a wide range of vegetarian mezze, salads, and mains. Al Hadheerah and Saffron have dedicated vegetarian stations.

### What is the typical iftar time in Dubai during Ramadan 2026?

Iftar is around 6:30 PM at the start of Ramadan, moving to about 7:00 PM by the end. Check daily prayer times for exact timing.

## The Bottom Line

The best iftar buffet in Dubai for 2026 depends on your budget and preference. For a luxurious experience, Al Hadheerah or Asado are top picks. For value and a view, Al Dawaar is a solid choice. If you want to save money, try local spots like Al Ustad Special Kabab. Book early, arrive on time, and enjoy the spirit of Ramadan.
