If you operate a commercial vehicle in California or travel through multiple IFTA member jurisdictions, you need to understand California's IFTA requirements. This guide covers who must register, how to file quarterly returns, what records to keep, and what penalties to avoid. Follow these steps to stay compliant and avoid costly fines.

## Who Must Register for IFTA in California?

You must register for IFTA if your vehicle has two axles and a gross vehicle weight rating (GVWR) or registered gross weight over 26,000 pounds, or if you have three or more axles regardless of weight, and you travel in two or more IFTA member jurisdictions. California is an IFTA member, so interstate travel triggers the requirement. If you only operate within California, you do not need IFTA.

New carriers must register with the California Department of Motor Vehicles (DMV) before operating interstate. The registration fee is $50 per vehicle, plus a $25 license fee for the carrier. You can register online through the DMV's IFTA portal or by mail. Expect processing to take 2 to 4 weeks, so plan ahead.

## How to File Quarterly IFTA Returns in California

IFTA returns are due quarterly, regardless of whether you had any miles or fuel purchases. The due dates are:

- Q1 (January-March): due April 30
- Q2 (April-June): due July 31
- Q3 (July-September): due October 31
- Q4 (October-December): due January 31

You must file even if you have zero miles. Filing is done online through the California DMV IFTA system. You will need your IFTA account number and records of miles traveled and fuel purchased in each jurisdiction. The return calculates net taxes owed or credits. If you owe taxes, payment is due with the return. If you have a credit, it carries forward to the next quarter.

## California Fuel Tax Rates for IFTA (2026)

California's fuel tax rate for IFTA is based on the average statewide diesel price. As of January 2026, the rate is approximately $0.39 per gallon for diesel. This rate is adjusted quarterly. For comparison, neighboring states like Oregon and Nevada have rates around $0.33 and $0.27 per gallon, respectively. Always check the current rate on the DMV IFTA website before filing.

## Record Keeping Requirements for California IFTA

You must maintain detailed records for at least 4 years from the due date of the return or the filing date, whichever is later. Required records include:

- Trip reports with date, origin, destination, route, and odometer readings
- Fuel receipts showing gallons, price, and location
- Vehicle mileage records by jurisdiction
- Fuel inventory records if you have bulk storage

Keep these records organized and accessible. The DMV can audit you at any time. Use a logbook or electronic logging device (ELD) to track miles, and save every fuel receipt, even for cash purchases.

## Penalties for Non-Compliance in California

Late filing or payment penalties are 10% of the tax due, plus interest at 1% per month. If you fail to file for multiple quarters, the DMV may suspend your IFTA license, which halts your interstate operations. Intentional evasion can result in fines up to $5,000 and criminal charges. Avoid these by filing on time, even if you owe nothing.

## Steps to Get Compliant This Week

1. **Check your registration status**: If you're new, apply for IFTA with the DMV immediately. If you're already registered, verify your account is active.
2. **Set up a record-keeping system**: Use a spreadsheet or software to track miles and fuel. Start with your current trip data.
3. **Review your fuel receipts**: Ensure you have receipts for all fuel purchases in the current quarter.
4. **Mark the filing deadline**: Put the next quarterly due date on your calendar.
5. **File your return**: If you're behind, file past due returns as soon as possible to minimize penalties.

## FAQ

**Q: Do I need IFTA if I only drive in California?**
A: No. IFTA applies only to interstate travel. If you stay within California, you don't need IFTA, but you still need California fuel tax permits for intrastate operations.

**Q: Can I file IFTA returns by mail?**
A: California requires online filing for all IFTA returns. You can access the system through the DMV website. Paper filing is not accepted.

**Q: What happens if I miss a quarterly deadline?**
A: You'll face a 10% penalty on any tax due, plus monthly interest. Even if you owe nothing, late filing can result in a $50 late fee.

**Q: How do I get a fuel tax credit for California?**
A: If you paid more fuel tax in a jurisdiction than you owe based on miles, you can claim a credit on your IFTA return. The credit carries forward to the next quarter.

## The Bottom Line

California IFTA requirements are straightforward if you stay organized. Register with the DMV, file quarterly returns on time, keep accurate records for 4 years, and pay attention to rate changes. The cost of compliance is low compared to penalties. Start by setting up a simple tracking system today, and you'll be ready for your next filing deadline.
