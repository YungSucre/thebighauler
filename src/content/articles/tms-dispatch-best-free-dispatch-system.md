If you are a small fleet owner or owner-operator looking to move beyond paper logs and phone calls, a free dispatch system can handle load assignment, driver communication, and basic tracking without eating into your margins. The best free options are not full-featured TMS platforms, but they cover the essentials: dispatching, status updates, and document management. This guide breaks down the top free dispatch systems available in 2026, what they actually include, and how to choose one that fits your operation.

## What to Look for in a Free Dispatch System

Before diving into specific tools, know what features matter most for dispatch. A free system should include:

- **Load assignment**: Create a load, assign a driver, and set pickup and delivery times.
- **Status tracking**: Drivers can update load status (empty, en route, arrived, delivered) from a mobile app.
- **Document capture**: Ability to upload bills of lading, receipts, and delivery photos.
- **Communication**: In-app messaging or notes tied to the load.
- **Reporting**: Basic reports on completed loads, revenue, and driver activity.

Free tiers often limit the number of drivers, loads, or active users. For a solo operator or a fleet of 2-5 trucks, these limits are usually fine. As you grow, you may need to upgrade to a paid plan, but starting free lets you test workflows without commitment.

## Top Free Dispatch Systems in 2026

Here are the most practical free dispatch systems, based on current offerings and user feedback. Prices and limits are as of early 2026 and can change, so verify on the vendor's website.

### 1. TruckLogics (Free Plan)

TruckLogics offers a free plan for owner-operators and small fleets. It includes dispatch, IFTA fuel tax calculation, and basic accounting. The free version allows up to 1 driver and 1 vehicle, with unlimited loads. You get access to the mobile app for drivers, document upload, and customer management. The free plan is a good starting point for a solo operator who wants a full TMS experience without cost.

**Limits**: 1 driver, 1 truck, unlimited loads. No broker integration or advanced reporting.

### 2. Axon Software (Free Trial, Not Free)

Axon is a robust TMS, but it does not have a permanent free tier. It offers a 30-day free trial with all features. After the trial, you pay $50-$70 per month per user. If you only need a system for a short project or want to test a professional-grade tool, the trial is useful. However, it is not a free dispatch system in the long term.

**Best for**: Fleets that plan to scale and want to test before paying.

### 3. SuperTruck (Free Plan)

SuperTruck is a newer entrant with a free plan designed for small fleets. It includes dispatch, GPS tracking, and driver app. The free tier supports up to 2 drivers and 2 vehicles, with unlimited loads. You can create loads, assign drivers, and track status in real time. It also offers document management and basic reporting. The interface is simple and mobile-friendly.

**Limits**: 2 drivers, 2 vehicles. No IFTA or factoring integration in the free plan.

### 4. Roswell (Free Plan)

Roswell is a TMS with a free plan for up to 3 drivers. It includes dispatch, electronic logging device (ELD) integration, and driver settlement. The free version allows unlimited loads and customers. You can send dispatch instructions to drivers via the mobile app, and drivers can update status. Roswell also offers factoring and fuel card integrations on paid plans, but the free tier is solid for basic dispatch.

**Limits**: 3 drivers, unlimited loads. No advanced reporting or custom fields.

### 5. Google Sheets + Free Apps (DIY Approach)

Many owner-operators use a spreadsheet for dispatch. You can create a load board with columns for load ID, customer, pickup, delivery, driver, and status. Pair it with a free app like Google Forms for drivers to submit updates, and Google Drive for document storage. This is not a dedicated dispatch system, but it costs nothing and can work for a single truck.

**Pros**: Total control, no limits. **Cons**: No automation, no mobile app, manual data entry.

## How to Choose the Right Free System for Your Operation

Follow these steps to pick a system that works for you:

1. **List your must-have features**: Do you need ELD integration? IFTA calculation? Factoring? If you only need basic dispatch, a simple tool like SuperTruck or Roswell will do.
2. **Check driver limits**: If you have 3 trucks, Roswell's 3-driver limit fits. If you have 5, you will need to upgrade or use a combination.
3. **Test with a real load**: Sign up for a free plan and run a test load. See how easy it is to assign a driver, how the driver app works, and whether status updates are intuitive.
4. **Consider future growth**: If you plan to add trucks, choose a system that scales. TruckLogics and Roswell have paid plans that grow with you.
5. **Read the fine print**: Some free plans require a credit card for activation or limit support. Avoid those if you want zero cost.

## Practical Steps to Get Started This Week

- **Day 1**: Choose one free system from the list. Sign up and create your company profile.
- **Day 2**: Enter your drivers and vehicles. If you have more than the limit, prioritize your active trucks.
- **Day 3**: Create a sample load and assign it to a driver. Test the mobile app.
- **Day 4**: Run a real load with the system. Have your driver update status at each stop.
- **Day 5**: Review the reports. See if the system gives you the information you need.

## FAQ

**Q: Are free dispatch systems really free?**  
A: Yes, the listed free plans have no monthly cost. However, they may have limits on drivers, vehicles, or features. Some require a credit card for verification, but you are not charged unless you upgrade.

**Q: Can I use a free dispatch system for IFTA reporting?**  
A: Some free plans, like TruckLogics, include basic IFTA fuel tax calculation. Others, like SuperTruck, do not. If IFTA is critical, choose a system that offers it or use a separate free fuel log.

**Q: Will a free dispatch system integrate with my ELD?**  
A: Roswell has ELD integration on its free plan. TruckLogics also offers ELD integration, but it may be a paid add-on. Check the vendor's integration list before committing.

**Q: What happens if I exceed the driver limit?**  
A: You will need to upgrade to a paid plan or remove inactive drivers. Some systems allow you to archive drivers, but you cannot have more active drivers than the limit.

## The Bottom Line

The best free dispatch system for you depends on your fleet size and needs. For a solo operator, TruckLogics offers a full TMS experience with IFTA. For a small fleet of up to 3 trucks, Roswell provides ELD integration and unlimited loads. SuperTruck is a solid choice for 2 trucks with GPS tracking. If you are just starting and want zero cost, a spreadsheet can work, but it lacks the convenience of a dedicated app. Start with a free plan, test it with real loads, and upgrade only when you outgrow the limits. That way, you get the benefits of a dispatch system without the upfront cost.
