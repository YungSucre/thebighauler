If you're an owner-operator or small fleet, you need to file IFTA quarterly. A free IFTA calculator can save you time and prevent costly errors. Here are the best free options available in 2026, plus how to use them correctly.

## What to Look for in a Free IFTA Calculator

A good IFTA calculator should:
- Track miles per state and gallons purchased per state.
- Calculate tax owed or credits per state.
- Allow manual entry or import from your ELD or fuel card.
- Generate a summary report you can use for filing.
- Be easy to use on a phone or laptop.

Free versions often have limits, like number of vehicles or trips. Know your needs before choosing.

## Top Free IFTA Calculators

### 1. TruckLogics (Free Plan)
TruckLogics offers a free plan that includes IFTA reporting. You can manually enter fuel and mileage, or import from supported ELDs. The free plan covers one vehicle and basic reporting. Paid plans start at $15/month for more features.

**Pros:** Easy to use, integrates with dispatch and maintenance.
**Cons:** Free plan limited to one truck.

### 2. ExpressIFTA (Free Trial)
ExpressIFTA is a dedicated IFTA software. They offer a 30-day free trial with full features. After that, pricing starts at $10/month per truck. It's not free forever, but the trial is useful for one quarter.

**Pros:** Specialized, accurate calculations.
**Cons:** Not free after trial.

### 3. IFTA Calculator by TruckingOffice (Free Tool)
TruckingOffice has a free online IFTA calculator that lets you enter miles and gallons per state. It calculates tax due or credit. It's a simple tool, not a full system. Great for quick checks.

**Pros:** No sign-up required, instant results.
**Cons:** No record keeping, manual entry only.

### 4. Fuel and Mileage Spreadsheet (Free Template)
Many trucking companies offer free IFTA spreadsheets. For example, the Apex Capital IFTA spreadsheet or the DAT IFTA template. You download an Excel file, enter data, and it calculates totals. This is 100% free and works offline.

**Pros:** No cost, customizable.
**Cons:** Manual data entry, risk of formula errors if you edit.

### 5. ELD Built-in IFTA Reports (Free with Your ELD)
Most ELD providers include IFTA reports in their subscription. For example, KeepTruckin (now Motive), Samsara, and EROAD offer IFTA mileage reports. You still need to add fuel data, but miles are automatic. Check if your ELD plan includes this.

**Pros:** No extra cost, accurate miles.
**Cons:** Fuel entry may be manual, reports may need export.

## How to Use a Free IFTA Calculator Effectively

1. **Gather your data.** You need total miles per state and total gallons purchased per state. Your ELD can give you miles. Fuel receipts or fuel card reports give you gallons.
2. **Enter data accurately.** Double-check state abbreviations and numbers. A typo can cause a big error.
3. **Calculate quarterly.** IFTA is filed quarterly: Jan-Mar, Apr-Jun, Jul-Sep, Oct-Dec. Deadlines are the last day of the month after the quarter ends.
4. **Review the summary.** The calculator should show net tax due or credit. If you have credit, you can apply it to next quarter.
5. **File your return.** Use the summary to complete your IFTA return on your state's portal. Some states accept electronic filing.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

- **Not including all miles.** Even empty miles count.
- **Forgetting fuel bought in one state but used in another.** You report gallons by where you bought them, not where you burned them.
- **Using outdated tax rates.** Rates change quarterly. Check the International Fuel Tax Agreement (IFTA) website for current rates.
- **Not keeping receipts.** You need to keep fuel receipts for at least 4 years.

## FAQ

### Is a free IFTA calculator accurate?
Yes, if you enter correct data. The math is simple: miles per gallon per state times tax rate. Free tools use the same formulas as paid ones.

### Can I file my IFTA return directly from a free calculator?
No, most free calculators only give you the numbers. You still need to file on your state's IFTA portal or by mail.

### What if I have multiple trucks?
Free plans often limit to one vehicle. For multiple trucks, consider a paid plan or use a spreadsheet that can handle more.

### Do I need a calculator if my ELD has IFTA reports?
Your ELD gives you miles, but you still need to add fuel data. A calculator or spreadsheet helps combine both.

## The Bottom Line

The best free IFTA calculator depends on your situation. If you have one truck and want a full system, TruckLogics free plan is solid. Free tools can save you money, but accuracy is your responsibility.
